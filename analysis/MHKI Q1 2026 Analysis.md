# Comprehensive Financial Report Review
## PT Multi Hanna Kreasindo Tbk (MHKI) — Q1 2026 (Interim, Unaudited)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Overall Financial Health: DETERIORATING — sharp earnings decline offsets balance sheet strength**

PT Multi Hanna Kreasindo Tbk (MHKI) reported a deeply disappointing Q1 2026, with revenue plunging **-28.4% YoY** to Rp34.8B and net income collapsing **-68.2%** to Rp3.5B compared to Q1 2025. The decline was driven by the apparent loss of PT PLN as a major customer (from 22.3% of revenue in Q1 2025 to 0% in Q1 2026), only partially offset by PT Pertamina emerging as a new 16% customer. Gross margin compressed **-650 bps** from 54.4% to 47.9%, while G&A expenses grew +9.6% despite the revenue contraction, creating severe operating deleverage. Operating cash flow turned negative at -Rp0.77B (vs +Rp27.08B in Q1 2025), driven by a +414.7% surge in supplier payments and a +93.2% inventory build-up.

On the positive side, the balance sheet remains exceptionally strong with a **net cash position of Rp104.4B**, a debt-to-equity ratio of only **4.6%**, and the company successfully collected receivables (trade receivables fell -39.5%). The Lamongan expansion appears to be entering its final stages, with Rp14.24B in construction-in-progress reclassified to completed buildings and Rp16.18B in new machinery added. However, the fundamental question is whether the capacity expansion can reverse the deteriorating revenue and margin trajectory. The stock has fallen **-33%** from ~Rp180 in April 2026 to Rp120 in July 2026, now trading below book value (P/B 0.93x).

**Suggestions:**

6. **P/E of 13.8x (TTM)** appears reasonable, but the annualized Q1 P/E of 30.6x shows that if Q1 performance persists, the stock is expensive. The market is likely pricing in a recovery in Q2-Q4 2026.

5. **Total Liabilities (-11.3%)**: The company is deleveraging, with both trade payables and bank loans declining. This is prudent but reduces financial flexibility if the revenue decline persists.

## 11. Overall Assessment & Recommendation

### Investment Thesis Summary

**MHKI is at a critical inflection point.** The company's Q1 2026 results reveal a sharp deterioration in both revenue (-28.4%) and profitability (-68.2% net income), driven primarily by the loss of PT PLN as a major customer and severe operating deleverage from fixed G&A costs. While the balance sheet remains exceptionally strong (net cash Rp104.4B, D/E 4.6%), the fundamental earnings power has been disrupted. The Lamongan expansion is nearing completion (Rp14.24B in buildings reclassified, Rp16.18B in new machinery added), but the question is whether new capacity can attract sufficient customers to replace the lost PLN revenue and drive a recovery. The stock has fallen -33% to Rp120, now trading below book value (P/B 0.93x).

### Price Target Framework

At Rp120/share (P/B 0.93x, P/E TTM 13.8x), the stock is trading below book value — a level that suggests the market is pricing in either: (a) permanent earnings deterioration, or (b) significant asset quality risk (inventory write-downs, stranded expansion assets).

**Bull case (recovery):** If the Lamongan facility comes online, PLN business returns or is replaced, and revenue recovers to Rp50B+/quarter with 20%+ operating margins:

The risk/reward at Rp120 is balanced. The strong balance sheet provides a floor (P/B 0.93x with net cash of Rp104.4B), but the earnings recovery is uncertain. **HOLD/WATCH** pending Q2 2026 results and Lamongan facility updates. A definitive buy signal would be revenue recovery above Rp45B/quarter with positive operating cash flow. A sell signal would be continued revenue decline below Rp30B/quarter or inventory write-downs.
