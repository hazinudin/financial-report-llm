# TPMA Tbk (TPMA) — Q2 2026
---

*All figures are reported in USD full units. The financial statements are unaudited interim statements for the period ended 30 June 2026. Quarterly income-statement and cash-flow comparisons are to the prior-year quarter (Q2 2025); balance-sheet comparisons are to 31 December 2025 fiscal year-end.*

## 1. Executive Summary

TPMA Tbk reported unaudited second-quarter 2026 revenue of USD 62.8M, a 15.9% year-on-year increase, yet the quarter was defined by severe profitability compression. Net profit collapsed 67.4% to USD 3.6M from USD 11.0M in Q2 2025, as gross profit fell 28.1% to USD 12.2M despite the top-line expansion. The deterministic gross margin printed at 19.46%, while COGS surged to USD 50.6M from USD 37.2M in the prior-year quarter, absorbing all revenue gains and more. Interest expense escalated to USD 4.1M from USD 2.8M, becoming the largest overhead item after COGS and driving pre-tax profit down to USD 4.4M from USD 11.7M. The computed effective tax rate is −17.29% per the ratio set, reflecting the sign convention of the source data.

The balance sheet weakened measurably over the six months to 30 June 2026. Cash and cash equivalents dropped to USD 10.0M from USD 16.2M at fiscal year-end, a drawdown that coincided with heavy capital expenditure, debt repayment, and dividend distributions. Current liabilities now exceed current assets, producing negative working capital of USD 1.9M and a current ratio of 0.958. Net debt stands at USD 87.7M, representing a net-debt-to-equity ratio of 0.612. Trade receivables expanded to USD 25.8M from USD 21.5M, pushing days-sales-outstanding to 37.5. Total loans of USD 98.4M against owners’ equity of USD 125.0M leave the group highly leveraged; interest coverage is not disclosed because operating profit is not mapped.

Cash flow from operations declined 35.5% to USD 13.9M, still positive but insufficient to fund the quarter’s simultaneous growth capex, debt amortization, and shareholder payouts. The deterministic free-cash-flow metric is USD 27.1M, computed as OCF less capex per the ratio definition. Financing outflows—loan repayments of USD 9.9M, interest paid of USD 4.1M, and dividends paid of USD 9.0M—absorbed the operating inflow and drove a net cash decrease of USD 6.2M. No market data or share-price information was provided; consequently, all market-based valuation multiples are not disclosed.

We assign a **HOLD (High Risk)** recommendation. TPMA exhibits revenue momentum and long-term contract visibility through 2028, but the combination of severe margin compression, negative working capital, and an aggressive capital-allocation posture suggests balance-sheet fragility. Without observable market multiples, valuation is opaque; the stock is appropriate only for risk-tolerant portfolios with a catalyst watch on refinancing and fleet delivery.

## 10. Strengths

### 10.1 Financial Strengths

- Operating cash flow remains positive at USD 13.9M despite the earnings collapse, with a cash-conversion ratio of 3.86x net profit.
- Equity base of USD 143.4M provides a buffer, and the equity ratio of 51.7% keeps total assets majority-owned.
- Collections ratio of 93.2% indicates that revenue is largely cash-backed, limiting receivables risk and revenue-quality concerns.

### 10.2 Operational Strengths

- Revenue grew 15.9% year-on-year, demonstrating volume or rate traction in the marine-logistics market.
- Long-term transport commitments extend through December 2028, offering near-term revenue visibility.
- Customer relationships with large coal producers (e.g., PT Kaltim Prima Coal, PT Indexim Coalindo) anchor the order book and provide a base-load utilization argument.

### 10.3 Strategic Strengths

- Majority-controlled subsidiaries BEST (65%) and TOP (51%) provide operational scale and captive capacity.
- Fleet renewal program via newbuild contracts should improve fuel efficiency and lifting capacity over the medium term.
- Related-party ecosystem (PT Samudra Investama Maju, PT Trans Logistik Perkasa) may offer preferential charter access, though governance risk accompanies this.

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks

- **HIGH — Liquidity Strain:** Current ratio of 0.958 and negative working capital of USD 1.9M mean short-term obligations exceed liquid resources. Cash dropped sharply in six months to USD 10.0M from USD 16.2M.
- **HIGH — Margin Compression:** Gross profit fell 28.1% on a 15.9% revenue increase. COGS inflation is not being passed through to customers, suggesting pricing pressure or input-cost pressure that management has not been able to recover via rates.
- **MODERATE — Interest Cost Spiral:** Interest expense rose to USD 4.1M from USD 2.8M YoY, consuming interest income and eroding pre-tax profit. With no disclosed operating profit, coverage cannot be assessed.

### 11.2 Structural Risks

- **HIGH — Customer Concentration:** The top three disclosed customers represent 29%, 13%, and 11% of sales respectively. Loss or renegotiation with PT Indexim Coalindo alone would remove nearly one-third of revenue.
- **MODERATE — Leverage:** Net debt of USD 87.7M and ND/E of 0.61x are elevated for a cyclical logistics business. Loans-to-equity of 0.69x and total D/E of 0.93x limit balance-sheet flexibility.
- **MODERATE — Capex Funding Gap:** Q2 capex of USD 13.2M plus dividends of USD 9.0M plus loan repayments of USD 9.9M collectively absorbed far more than the quarter’s OCF of USD 13.9M. The gap is funded by drawing down cash and potentially increasing borrowings.

### 11.3 Market and Regulatory Risks

- **MODERATE — Geopolitical and Macro:** Note 30 references escalated geopolitical tensions following military actions at end-February 2026, but the context note flags a date inconsistency given the 30 June 2026 reporting date. Regardless, coal-export demand and bunker-fuel prices remain exposed to regional stability.
- **LOW — Tax and Regulatory:** Taxable income is shown as “-” for both periods, and the effective tax rate is low/absorptive. A change in tax treatment or final-tax regime could alter cash flows, but this is a low-probability near-term concern.

### 11.4 Valuation Concerns

No market data are available. Without observable P/E, EV/EBITDA, or yield, investors lack a market-implied risk premium benchmark. The low absolute EPS of USD 0.0009 and BVPS of USD 0.0354 suggest a micro-cap or deeply discounted structure, but this is speculative without a price.
