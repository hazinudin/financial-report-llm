# SRSN Tbk (SRSN) — Q1 2026
---

## 1. Executive Summary

SRSN’s Q1 2026 unaudited interim statements—reported in thousand Indonesian Rupiah—present a stark divergence between top-line contraction and bottom-line expansion. Revenue fell to Rp239.5bn from Rp312.5bn in Q1 2025, a pre-computed decline of 23.34%, yet gross profit rose to Rp74.5bn from Rp63.3bn as COGS declined materially, resetting the margin structure. Operating profit more than doubled to Rp38.6bn and net profit nearly tripled to Rp28.9bn, translating to basic EPS of Rp4.80 against Rp1.62 in the prior-year quarter. The balance sheet underwent aggressive deleveraging: current bank loans dropped to Rp20.0bn from Rp95.0bn at the prior fiscal year-end, total liabilities decreased to Rp172.6bn from Rp242.6bn, and cash climbed to Rp16.2bn from Rp6.3bn despite heavy debt amortization. Operating cash flow swung from a Rp19.2bn outflow in Q1 2025 to a Rp87.7bn inflow, yielding core free cash flow of Rp92.2bn after only Rp4.5bn of capex. With no market data provided, valuation multiples are not disclosed; the inability to assess entry pricing against this earnings inflection leaves the rating qualified. We assign a HOLD (Qualified) pending an observable market price and confirmation that the margin expansion is sustainable beyond a one-off cost windfall. Key watch items are the Q2 revenue trajectory, finalization of the extended BCA facility, and the decades-old commercial paper overhang.

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price

The fact sheet provides no production volume or realized price per liter for ethanol or acetic acid, so a volume-versus-price decomposition is not disclosed. Segment-level external sales indicate ethanol contributed Rp208.2bn, acetic acid Rp21.6bn, and other activities Rp9.7bn. No foreign-exchange tailwind is quantified, although Note 12 discloses USD-denominated trade payables of USD 6.1m within total payables, implying some natural FX exposure but no disclosed translation gain on revenue.

### 2.3 Revenue Composition Observations

Trade receivables reveal customer concentration risk: the three largest disclosed exposures are PT Nippon Shokubai Indonesia (Rp29.9bn), Tanduay Distillers, Inc (Rp24.7bn), and Pinnacle Synergy Pte. Ltd. (Rp23.9bn). Related-party receivables total Rp812.6m, split between PT Sama Mandiri (Rp734.3m) and PT Sari Warna Asli Textile Industri (Rp78.3m). Context notes flag that sales to related parties represent only 0.97% of total sales, meaning the receivables concentration is not matched by a commensurate revenue share and collection profiles should be monitored. Domestic versus external revenue splits are not disclosed beyond the segment external sales lines.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

The only disclosed forward volume commitment is molasses feedstock procurement of 152,100 tons for 2026 versus 124,000 tons for 2025, contracted with PT Kebon Agung, PTPN IX–XII, and PT PG Rajawali II. This secures raw-material runway for ethanol but does not guarantee offtake pricing. No post-period-end commodity price moves, reserve upgrades, or capacity expansions were disclosed. The critical watch item is whether Q1’s Rp239.5bn revenue run-rate reflects a demand trough or a structural step-down in ethanol pricing; without volume data, management commentary on order book visibility would be necessary but is not provided.
