# Equity Research Report: PT Archi Indonesia Tbk (IDX: ARCI)
## FY2025 Results Analysis

---

## 1. Executive Summary

PT Archi Indonesia Tbk (ARCI) delivered an exceptional FY2025, with revenue surging 72.5% YoY to **US$496.2M** and net profit attributable to parent exploding 873% to **US$101.8M**, driven primarily by the historic rally in gold prices. However, underlying operational cash flow declined, leverage has increased materially, and regulatory headwinds are intensifying. We assign a cautious outlook despite the strong headline results.

## 3. External Factor Analysis

### 3.1 Gold Price Environment (Strongly Positive)

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| Gold price (2025) | Surged ~50-60%, surpassing US$4,000/oz for the first time |
| J.P. Morgan forecast | US$5,000/oz by end of 2026; US$5,400/oz by end of 2027 |
| Morgan Stanley forecast | US$4,400/oz for 2026 (revised up from US$3,313) |
| World Gold Council outlook | 2026 rangebound-to-moderately-bullish; gold up 60%+ in 2025 |
| Key drivers | Central bank buying, geopolitical risk, USD weakness, rate cuts |

**Implication:** ARCI is a direct leveraged play on gold prices. The 2025 financial performance is dominated by gold's rally. However, gold prices at current levels are already pricing in significant risk premiums — any reversal in gold would disproportionately impact earnings given the high operating leverage.

### 3.2 Indonesia Mining Royalty Increase (Negative)

In April 2025, Indonesia implemented a **progressive gold royalty scheme**, increasing rates from **3.75%-10% to 7%-16%** based on gold price brackets. For ARCI:
- FY2025 royalties paid: **US$65.2M** (13.1% of revenue) vs US$23.5M (8.2% of revenue) in FY2024
- This has already taken a substantial bite — royalty-to-revenue ratio jumped from ~8.2% to ~13.1%
- At higher gold prices, the progressive bracket pushes royalties toward the 16% ceiling, further compressing margins

### 3.3 Indonesia Mining Law Revision — UU Minerba (Mixed)

The revised Mining law (Feb 2025) introduces:
- Priority for SMEs and cooperatives in mining concessions
- Reinforced downstream processing requirements
- Continued 51% divestment requirement for foreign-owned miners
- More discretionary licensing regime

**Implication for ARCI:** ARCI's concessions at Toka Tindung (~39,817 hectares) appear secure under existing Contracts of Work, but future expansion or new licenses face a more restrictive environment. The forced divestment regime remains a structural overhang.

### 3.4 Parent Company Stake Sale (Neutral/Negative)

In June 2025, Bloomberg reported that **PT Rajawali Corpora halted talks to sell its 85% stake** in ARCI. This suggests:
- A strategic sale is possible in the future if gold prices remain elevated
- Current ownership uncertainty may limit strategic investment decisions
- The free float is only ~8.82%, creating liquidity constraints for institutional investors

### 3.5 Exploration Investment (Positive)

ARCI invested **IDR 168.53 billion (~US$10M)** in exploration at Toka Tindung throughout 2025. As of 2020, mineral resources stood at 5,528 koz Au and ore reserves at 3,884 koz Au. Continued exploration spending is critical for mine life extension, particularly at current production rates.

### 3.6 Macroeconomic & FX Considerations

- ARCI reports in USD, but incurs costs primarily in IDR — **IDR weakness relative to USD** benefits the cost structure when translating local-currency costs
- The DHE (Devisa Hasil Ekspor) regulation requires mining companies to repatriate export proceeds, impacting liquidity management
- Indonesia's GDP growth remains stable (~5%), but inflation and logistics costs remain concerns

## 6. Key Risks

1. **Gold Price Sensitivity (Critical):** Every US$100/oz change in gold price has an outsized impact on earnings given the high fixed-cost base. The stock is a leveraged gold proxy.
2. **Regulatory Risk (High):** The new progressive royalty regime (7%-16%) will increasingly erode margins as gold prices rise. The revised mining law introduces ownership and licensing uncertainty.
3. **Cash Flow vs. Earnings Divergence (High):** Operating cash flow declining while reported profits surge raises questions about earnings quality and working capital management.
4. **Balance Sheet Leverage (Moderate-High):** Net debt of ~US$465M with increasing long-term borrowings. Finance costs of US$40.2M/year consume ~19.7% of operating income.
5. **Liquidity Risk (Moderate):** 8.82% free float makes the stock ill-suited for large institutional positions. Potential parent company exit could flood the market.
6. **Mine Reserve Depletion (Long-term):** Latest published reserves (2020) show 3,884 koz Au. At current production rates, mine life is a key concern requiring ongoing exploration success.

## 7. Conclusion & Recommendation

**Rating: HOLD / CAUTIOUS**

ARCI's FY2025 results are exceptional on the surface but reveal significant underlying concerns:

- **The earnings surge is almost entirely gold-price driven**, not structural. While this is positive in a rising gold environment, it creates acute downside risk if gold reverses.
- **Operating cash flow declined 42% despite an 884% profit increase**, indicating working capital absorption and higher cash taxes/royalties that are not fully captured in the income statement.
- **The progressive royalty regime** (7%-16%) acts as an automatic margin squeeze at higher gold prices, directly countering the operating leverage benefit.
- **Leverage is increasing** with net debt growing US$68M YoY, and finance costs remaining a heavy burden at US$40.2M.
- **Valuation at ~24x P/E** appears to fully price in the gold rally, leaving limited upside unless gold continues strongly into 2026.

The investment thesis depends almost entirely on gold sustaining above US$3,500/oz. For investors bullish on gold (J.P. Morgan targets US$5,000/oz by end-2026), ARCI offers leveraged exposure. For those cautious on gold's sustainability at current levels, the risk-reward is unfavorable given the leverage, negative free cash flow, and regulatory headwinds.
