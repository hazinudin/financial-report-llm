# PT Indosat Tbk (ISAT) — Q2 2026
---

*All financial figures are stated in millions of Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) unless otherwise noted. EPS is reported in full Rupiah per share. The underlying statements are unaudited interim financials for the six months ended 30 June 2026, with income-statement and cash-flow comparatives for the six months ended 30 June 2025 and balance-sheet comparatives as at 31 December 2025.*

## 1. Executive Summary

ISAT delivered a powerful H1 2026 earnings inflection, with revenue rising 13.1% year-on-year to IDR 30,653 billion and net profit attributable to owners surging 84.5% to IDR 4,309 billion. Operating profit accelerated 49.3% to IDR 7,738 billion, driving operating margin from 19.1% to 25.2%. The step-up was not volume-led—cellular revenue grew in line with the consolidated top-line—suggesting a sharp improvement in cost absorption, network opex leverage, or mix shift toward higher-yield data services. Pre-tax profit jumped 82.0% to IDR 5,613 billion, supported by a 23.1% increase in interest income, while finance costs rose only 5.1% despite a larger debt load, producing interest coverage of 3.36x. Earnings per share for the period came in at IDR 133.61 versus IDR 72.41 in H1 2025.

The balance sheet transformed dramatically. Cash and cash equivalents ballooned 211% from year-end 2025 to IDR 15,782 billion, lifting current assets by 37.1% and total assets by 16.6% to IDR 138,306 billion. Yet liquidity remains structurally tight: the current ratio sits at 0.67x and working capital is deeply negative at IDR –13,123 billion, with current liabilities of IDR 39,186 billion outweighing current assets. Total liabilities reached IDR 98,211 billion, pushing the debt-to-equity ratio to 2.45x. The cash surge is only partially explained by operating cash flow of IDR 11,764 billion and free cash flow of IDR 6,091 billion; the financing cash flow section in the disclosed stub is incomplete, leaving an unreconciled gap that implies material unreported debt drawdowns or inflows during the period.

Valuation multiples cannot be assessed because no market data stub was provided. On fundamentals alone, ISAT exhibits strong cash conversion (2.66x net profit to OCF) and a defendable cellular franchise, but the combination of negative working capital, near-term loan maturities (including a UOB term loan facility maturing November 2026), and opacity in financing cash flows keeps the risk profile elevated. The 5G spectrum license effective August 2026 provides a decade of regulatory runway, yet it also commits the company to an initial fee of IDR 879 billion and annual frequency charges of IDR 785 billion. We rate the stock HOLD (Qualified) pending closure of the financing cash flow reconciliation and Q3 evidence that Q2 margin expansion was operational rather than non-recurring.

## 2. Revenue Analysis

### 2.1 Revenue Performance

| Period | Revenue (IDR mn) | YoY Change |

| H1 2026 | 30,653,145 | +13.07% |

Revenue growth of 13.1% was driven entirely by the cellular and MIDI segments; fixed telecommunications contributed less than 1.5% of the total. No specific subscriber, ARPU, or traffic volume metrics were disclosed in the canonical notes, preventing a volume-versus-price decomposition.

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price

Segment disclosures show cellular external sales of IDR 25,527 billion (83.3% of revenue), MIDI at IDR 4,694 billion (15.3%), and fixed telecommunications at IDR 432 billion (1.4%). The cellular segment’s asset base expanded to IDR 105,764 billion, confirming that the top-line remains captive to mobile data and voice economics. The revenue table in the notes discloses a cellular discount of IDR 1,365 billion applied to gross cellular revenue, up from IDR 1,300 billion in H1 2025, implying that gross cellular revenue growth was slightly faster than the net 13.1% consolidated print. FX sensitivity is modest: a 0.26% USD appreciation would add IDR 4,543 million to profit, but the company runs a net USD liability position of 123,921 thousand USD as at 30 June 2026, so IDR weakening is a mixed bag.

### 2.3 Revenue Composition Observations

Related-party revenue totaled approximately IDR 1,199 billion in H1 2026, or 3.9% of consolidated revenue. The largest related-party revenue streams were state-owned companies (IDR 931 billion), state-owned banks (IDR 201 billion), Ooredoo (IDR 45 billion), and Hutchison (IDR 18 billion). Customer concentration beyond this is not disclosed. Domestic operations dominate; no geographic revenue split was provided.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

The 5G spectrum license, effective 5 August 2026 for a ten-year term to 4 August 2036, anchors the medium-term revenue runway but adds regulatory cost commitments: an initial fee of IDR 879 billion and annual frequency fees of IDR 785 billion. Capital expenditure commitments for telecom equipment and services stand at IDR 17,649 billion (IDR 14,777 billion undelivered), primarily with Huawei, Aivres System, Nokia, and ZTE, ensuring network densification continues through 2026–2027. Google Cloud minimum commitments (IDR 786 million) and associated A2P routing revenue (IDR 76 million recognized in H1) remain marginal. The key watch item is whether MIDI can sustain its IDR 4.7 trillion revenue contribution amid enterprise IT margin pressure.

### 7.2 The Core Operating Business

Cellular is the crown jewel: IDR 25,527 billion in external sales (83.3% of consolidated revenue) and IDR 105,764 billion in segment assets (76.5% of total assets). No segment-level profit is disclosed, but the consolidated operating margin of 25.2% is almost certainly driven by cellular economics. The 5G spectrum license secured in August 2026 underpins the next investment cycle. Radio frequency fees paid in H1 totaled IDR 1,344 billion.

### 7.3 Strategic Question Marks

Three items cloud the asset picture. First, PT Multi Media Asia Indonesia (MMAI) is no longer operating and is fully impaired (carrying value zero). Second, the IFT deconsolidation in H1 2026 removed fiber infrastructure from direct control and replaced it with a 49.9% JV in NFT; lease liabilities to IFT surged to IDR 11,302 billion, implying the group now rents rather than owns a large portion of that fiber footprint. Third, the Zankore Hypercloud entities (ZHPL, ZHSPL, ZHI) were established administratively in 2026 with no capital injection and zero assets, suggesting they are shells awaiting future AI or cloud deals.

### 7.4 Growth Options

The data center JV structure—NFT (49.9%) and SMT (25%)—represents the clearest second leg. NFT was established in March 2026 and already carries IDR 14,914 billion in assets. SMT holds IDR 10,975 billion. Together they account for IDR 25,889 billion, or 18.7% of consolidated assets, yet they contribute minimally to revenue today. PT Cakrawala Data Global (75%) is a small AI-based data processing bet with only IDR 10 billion in assets. The Google Cloud partnership and Huawei managed services (IDR 651 billion cost in H1) are operational enablers rather than standalone revenue engines.

### 7.5 Intercompany and Related-Party Dynamics

Related-party balances are large and must be monitored. Due from related parties totaled IDR 663 billion (SMT IDR 528 billion, state-owned companies IDR 128 billion). Due to related parties were minor at IDR 39 billion. Lease liabilities to related parties totaled IDR 16,184 billion (IFT IDR 11,302 billion, state-owned companies IDR 4,824 billion, SMT IDR 2,058 billion). Procurement payables to related parties fell sharply to IDR 61 billion from IDR 118 billion, suggesting a shift to third-party vendor financing or direct payments.

## 10. Strengths

### 10.1 Financial Strengths

Operating cash flow of IDR 11,764 billion and free cash flow of IDR 6,091 billion demonstrate robust cash generation. The cash conversion ratio of 2.66x and collections ratio of 97.3% indicate high earnings quality. Interest coverage of 3.36x provides a buffer above near-term debt service, and the cash balance of IDR 15,782 billion is the largest on record in the provided data series.

### 10.2 Operational Strengths

Cellular segment dominance (83% of revenue, 77% of assets) provides scale economies in network opex and procurement. Days sales outstanding of 11 days and a cash conversion cycle under 8 days are best-in-class for a consumer-facing telecom, minimizing working capital drag. ## 14. Investment Thesis

### 14.1 Bull Case

If Q2 margin expansion proves sustainable, ISAT is on track for a step-change in annualized ROE approaching 50%. 5G monetization, data center JVs (NFT/SMT), and AI/cloud optionality could re-rate the narrative from a legacy telco to a digital infrastructure play. ### 14.2 Bear Case

A refinancing cliff in H2 2026—UOB loan, bonds due 2026–2027—could strain liquidity if capital markets tighten. NCI drag at Lintasarta and structural fixed-line losses may erode consolidated margins. Regulatory fee creep (5G annual charges, radio fees) could absorb operating leverage.

### 14.3 Key Catalysts

- Q3 2026 interim results (October/November 2026): evidence of margin sustainability and cellular ARPU trends.
- Refinancing closure for the UOB term loan and 2027 bond maturities.
- 5G commercial service launch and associated tariff uptake.
- MIDI segment profitability turnaround or further impairment.
