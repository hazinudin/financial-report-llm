# MSTI Tbk (MSTI) — Q2 2026
---

**Reporting Currency:** Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) — full values. **Audit Status:** Unaudited interim financial statements for the six months ended June 30, 2026; comparative balance sheet figures are audited as at December 31, 2025. Comparative income statement and cash flow figures are for the six months ended June 30, 2025 (unaudited).

## 1. Executive Summary

MSTI delivered a bifurcated 1H26: the income statement shows stability while the cash flow statement and balance sheet reveal acute liquidity absorption. Revenue reached Rp 2,102,059,311,357, up 1.50% year-on-year per computed ratios. Net profit improved 4.40% to Rp 205,463,544,967, translating to basic EPS of Rp 65.45 (up 4.40%). Gross margin held at 18.34% and net margin at 9.77%. Yet operating cash flow was deeply negative at Rp (365,263,576,997), yielding a cash conversion ratio of negative 1.78x. Cash and cash equivalents collapsed from Rp 1,627,326,133,404 at year-end to Rp 932,278,996,083 at June 30, 2026.

The balance sheet contracted across the board. Total assets fell 8.71% per computed ratios, driven by a Rp 695 billion cash drawdown and an expanding working capital footprint. Trade receivables increased from Rp 788,231,006,195 to Rp 877,255,075,089, inventories surged from Rp 517,752,499,115 to Rp 773,302,514,492, and prepaid taxes rose from Rp 222,446,634,756 to Rp 283,000,222,465. On the liability side, trade payables decreased from Rp 733,005,383,246 to Rp 668,335,371,892 and other advances from customers fell from Rp 128,530,684,418 to Rp 63,760,891,074. Equity contracted 5.33% to Rp 2,169,360,617,053. Despite this, formal bank leverage remains negligible—loans-to-equity stands at 0.013x—and the current ratio is 3.42x.

Profitability is stable but not expanding. Gross profit grew only 0.44% per computed ratios, meaning nearly all revenue growth was absorbed by COGS. Other income net increased from Rp 23,538,308,120 to Rp 31,759,492,508, providing a material lift to pre-tax profit. Interest expense rose from Rp 3,086,176,879 to Rp 4,550,553,137. Operating profit is not disclosed as a line item, preventing operating margin and interest coverage assessment.

Capital allocation prioritizes shareholder payouts over liquidity retention. Cash dividends paid were Rp 332,778,117,200, down from Rp 370,451,111,600 in 1H25, but still compare to net profit of Rp 205,463,544,967. Capex was minimal at Rp 17,176,768,393. The company repaid its PT Bank Mandiri short-term facility in full and continued amortizing PT Bank Central Asia and PT Bank OCBC NISP term debt.

No market data—price, market capitalization, or EV—were provided, rendering valuation multiples unassessable. Management states the group has only one reportable segment, so no segment or subsidiary detail is disclosed. We rate the stock **HOLD — Cash-Flow Watch** pending a working capital inflection and market-price disclosure.
