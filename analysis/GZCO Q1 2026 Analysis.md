# GZCO Tbk (GZCO) — Q1 2026
---

## 1. Executive Summary

GZCO (PT Golden Zaga Indonesia Tbk) reported a sharply deteriorated Q1 2026 interim result (unaudited) versus the prior-year quarter. Consolidated revenue collapsed 35.4% to IDR 139,426 million from IDR 215,689 million in Q1 2025, while the group swung from a gross profit of IDR 36,849 million to a gross loss of IDR 1,216 million (gross margin -0.87% vs prior implied 17.09%). The deterioration flowed through to a net loss of IDR 5,063 million (net margin -3.63%) against a net profit of IDR 7,072 million a year earlier, with basic EPS flipping to a loss of IDR 0.82 from a profit of IDR 1.11. The loss was amplified by elevated selling (IDR 8,248 million) and G&A (IDR 16,256 million) expenses, partially offset by a large other-income net line of IDR 26,243 million (including fair-value gain on biological assets of IDR 25,112 million per context note 34).

The balance sheet as of 31 March 2026 shows total assets of IDR 2,169,656 million, down marginally from IDR 2,191,904 million at December 2025 (prior fiscal year-end). Equity contracted slightly to IDR 1,226,497 million (equity owners IDR 1,177,054 million; NCI IDR 49,443 million). Crucially, current assets of IDR 273,421 million are dwarfed by current liabilities of IDR 484,929 million, producing negative working capital of IDR 211,508 million and a current ratio of merely 0.56x. Cash fell to IDR 98,230 million from IDR 125,345 million, but the group remains net cash on a loans‑minus‑cash‑minus‑securities basis (net debt IDR -9,204 million) thanks to the large related‑party loan from the President Director (IDR 200,000 million) being unsecured and long‑term in nature.

Operating cash flow strengthened materially to IDR 59,665 million (vs IDR 32,534 million prior year), a 83.4% increase, driven by collections of IDR 154,287 million (collections ratio 1.11x revenue). Free cash flow of IDR 37,430 million (after total capex IDR 22,235 million) underscores underlying cash generation despite the P&L loss. However, the group repaid IDR 66,034 million of loans and paid IDR 4,627 million interest, leading to a net cash decline of IDR 27,115 million.

Valuation cannot be assessed: the market‑data stub was not provided, so P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA and target price are not disclosed. Based on the weak earnings trajectory, negative gross margin, and concentrated customer/related‑party exposures, we assign a HOLD (HIGH RISK) rating pending clarity on commodity price recovery and the pending disposal of PT Palma Sejahtera. The crown‑jewel plantation assets (15,668 Ha planted, NBV IDR 857,494 million) remain pledged as collateral under the BSI musyarakah facility with covenants (current ratio ≥1.00x, D/E ≤3.00x, DSCR ≥1.00x) that are currently breached on the current‑ratio test.
