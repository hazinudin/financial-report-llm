# Ammonia Price Forecast Update (July 17, 2026)

## Executive Summary

The ammonia market has undergone a **dramatic narrative reversal** in the three weeks since the June 17 US-Iran MOU. The "war premium unwinding" thesis — which drove urea futures down −47 to −53% from the April peak and ammonia down −10.8% through June — has been **re-reversed** by the July 8 ceasefire collapse and the July 11 IRGC strike on M/V GFS Galaxy. Iran has **formally re-declared the Strait of Hormuz closed until further notice** (first formal closure declaration of the conflict), the US has launched a third round of strikes on ~140 Iranian targets, and Iran's retaliation has widened to Jordan, Qatar, Bahrain, and the UAE. Qatar's Transport Ministry issued the first blanket Gulf-state maritime suspension, directly curtailing Ras Laffan LNG exports. Urea (CFD) has already surged **+17.78% MoM to $424/t** (TradingEconomics, Jul 16), with NOLA spot (~$453/t pre-escalation) watching a break above $500/t. The ammonia index (IMARC) lags urea by ~2–4 weeks; the July print (expected early August) will be the first hard confirmation, but the directional signal is unambiguous: **the April–May peak is no longer the cycle top**.

The **dual catalysts** for the current re-surge:
1. **Geopolitical Re-Escalation**: The July 8 ceasefire collapse and July 11 IRGC SoH re-closure have shattered the June "normalization" narrative. Crisis pressure on straits.live hit 91 (extreme) on Jul 17; Windward operational risk = **CRITICAL**.
2. **Structural LNG Floor Confirmed**: Qatar's Ras Laffan LNG & fertilizer hub damage has been confirmed by S&P Global and Maritime Executive as requiring **3–5 years to fully repair**. Even under the brief June "quick reopening" window, briefs.co (Jul 5) projected urea would stay 35–56% above pre-crisis through fall 2026, with a long-run floor of $532/short ton (~13% above pre-crisis). The structural floor persists **regardless of any individual ceasefire oscillation**.

**Current Status (as of July 17, 2026):**
- NE Asia ammonia: estimated **~$0.34–0.36/kg** (lagged from urea re-surge; IMARC July release pending ~early Aug)
- Urea (CFD): **$424/t** (+17.78% MoM) — first responder to re-escalation
- Urea (NOLA spot): **~$453/t** pre-escalation; break above $500/t watched (ChemicalsBlog Jul 13)
- Strait of Hormuz: **formally closed by IRGC** (Jul 11); only ~21 transits/day vs 100–138 pre-war
- Qatar maritime: **blanket suspension** by Transport Ministry (Jul 11, Windward)
- USDIDR: **~18,000+** (Jul 14 high 18,177; Jul 16 17,962) — reinforcing ESSA's dual tailwind

## 3. The "Oscillating Crisis" — A New Market Regime

Unlike the 2022 Russia-Ukraine shock (one-directional structural step-up), the 2026 US-Iran conflict is exhibiting an **oscillating pattern**:

| Phase | Dates | Price Action | SoH Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **War shock** | Feb 28 – Apr 8 | Urea +85% Apr peak; Ammonia +23% Apr peak | SoH effectively closed |
| **Ceasefire** | Apr 8 – Jun 17 | Prices peak, then plateau | Ceasefire holds; some transit resumes |
| **MOU / reopening** | Jun 17 – Jul 8 | Urea −47 to −53% from peak; Ammonia −10.8% | 284 vessels transit Jun 18+; toll-free 60-day window |
| **Re-escalation** | Jul 8 – present | Urea +17.78% MoM (Jul 16); Ammonia re-rising (est.) | Ceasefire collapses Jul 8; IRGC re-closes SoH Jul 11 |

### Implications of the Oscillating Regime

- **Spot traders whipsawed**: Buying-the-trough (Jun) on "normalization" thesis has been decisively stopped out within ~3 weeks
- **Structural floors ratchet up**: Each oscillation leaves a higher baseline insurance, freight, and risk premium. War-risk insurance at 8.0× pre-crisis (straits.live, Jul 17)
- **Forecast uncertainty rises**: Single-scenario point forecasts are less useful than **range forecasts** anchored by the structural floor ($532/st urea long-run minimum per briefs.co) and the spike ceiling (~$900+/st if extended conflict materializes per NDSU)
- **H2 2026 base case must be revised UP**: The [[ESSA_Q2_2026_Earnings_Forecast]] "slow normalization" assumption (Q3 ~$600/MT, Q4 ~$500/MT) now looks too pessimistic — management's 3–6 month normalization guidance is likely a **floor**, not a ceiling, under realized pricing

## 9. Conclusion

The ammonia market has transitioned from a **"normalization" regime** (Jun 2026) back to a **"re-escalation / oscillating crisis" regime** (Jul 2026). The June "war premium unwind" has been re-reversed within ~3 weeks by the IRGC's formal re-closure of the Strait of Hormuz and the US third-round strikes. Urea — the faster-moving nitrogen proxy — has already surged +17.78% MoM. Ammonia, which lags urea by ~2–4 weeks in index data, is estimated to be re-rising in the ~$0.34–0.36/kg range (NE Asia), with the IMARC July print (expected early August) as the first hard confirmation.

**Three possible paths from here:**

1. **Sustained closure / regional war** (probability ~35%): SoH remains closed 6+ months; Qatar Ras Laffan suspension extends; Iran retaliation widens further. Urea pushes toward NDSU's "Extended Conflict" $996/st by Oct; ammonia follows with lag. ESSA's H2 realized price could average **$650–$750/MT**.

2. **Contested / fragile transit** (probability ~45%): Oman route-management proposal gains partial traction; SoH reopens intermittently under toll/escort regime; Qatar partial LNG restart by late 2026. Urea oscillates $450–$650/st; ammonia $0.34–0.42/kg. ESSA H2 realized **$550–$650/MT**.

3. **Rapid diplomatic breakthrough** (probability ~20%): Iran accepts verified nuclear curbs + sanctions sequencing; SoH reopens within 4–6 weeks under new international administration; Qatar repairs accelerate. Urea reverts toward $400–$450/st; ammonia $0.30–0.34/kg. ESSA H2 realized **$450–$550/MT**.

**Base case:** Ammonia NE Asia averages **$0.36–0.42/kg through H2 2026** (~$550–$700/MT SE Asia reference), with urea oscillating $450–$650/st. The structural floor from Qatar Ras Laffan (3–5 yr repair) means even the "rapid breakthrough" scenario leaves prices **materially above pre-crisis** ($0.30/kg / ~$480/MT SE Asia). The "oscillating crisis" pattern implies higher volatility but a higher mean price than the June "normalization" thesis assumed.

**Strategic recommendation for ESSA:** The June "champion stock under pressure" narrative has been **re-validated** by July re-escalation. Both tailwinds — ammonia price and Rupiah — are now moving in the same direction. The H2 earnings base case likely needs upward revision from the ~$550/MT assumption in [[ESSA_Q2_2026_Earnings_Forecast]] to ~$600–$650/MT. With zero debt, ~$195M projected cash, and post-turnaround +9% production uplift, ESSA is positioned to capture the windfall across a wider volume base. The blue ammonia feasibility ($120–150M, end-2026) is strategically more valuable than ever as Japan accelerates non-Gulf supply diversification.
