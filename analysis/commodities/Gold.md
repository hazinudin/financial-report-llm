## 3. Supply Side

| Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Annual mine supply** | ~2,500–3,500 metric tonnes/year (1–1.5% growth) |
| **Recycling** | Increasing at elevated prices; partially offsets mine constraints |
| **Central bank reserves** | Structural shift toward gold diversification; not cyclical |
| **ETF holdings** | US fund outflows in March 2026 suggest profit-taking |

Unlike base metals (e.g., [[Aluminium]]), gold's supply is relatively inelastic. The rally is **demand-driven**, not supply-constrained.

## 6. Macro Environment

### Interest Rate Outlook
- **CME June cut probability**: 5.1% (to 3.25–3.50%) — low probability limits near-term upside
- **Fed dissent**: 4 policymakers dissented in May 2026 meeting, signaling policy divide over Iran conflict
- **Real rates**: ~1.15% (nominal 4.45% – inflation 3.3%) — neutral-to-slightly-negative for gold

### Geopolitical Risk
- **Middle East conflict**: Ongoing; Strait of Hormuz near-total closure
- **US-Iran tensions**: US naval blockade until nuclear deal reached
- **Trade concerns**: Ongoing tariff disputes reducing USD demand

Both factors embed a geopolitical risk premium in gold prices, similar to their impact on [[Aluminium]] and [[Ammonia]] markets.

## 7. Gold in the Indonesian Context

### ARCI — Primary Gold Producer
- **Q1 2026 production**: 29 koz (+38% YoY) — strongest Q1 on record
- **FY 2026 forecast**: 155–168 koz (base case ~160 koz)
- **Revenue impact**: At ~$4,700/oz, FY 2026 revenue could exceed $750M
- **Seasonal pattern**: Q1 trough (0.69x avg), Q3 peak (1.21x avg) — driven by Indonesia's monsoon cycle in North Sulawesi

### Regulatory Headwinds
- **Progressive gold royalty**: 7%–16% based on price brackets (up from 3.75%–10%)
- At current gold prices, ARCI faces the top bracket, compressing margins
- Revised mining law introduces ownership and licensing uncertainty

### Investment Thesis
The [[ARCI keystats robertgunawan Stockbit post]] analysis highlights a long-term thesis of gold reaching $8,000/oz over several years. At current levels (~$4,700), this implies ~70% upside from here. ARCI offers leveraged exposure — every $100/oz change in gold price has an outsized impact on earnings given the high fixed-cost base.
