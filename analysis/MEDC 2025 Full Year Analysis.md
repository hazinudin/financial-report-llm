PT Medco Energi Internasional Tbk (MEDC) completed 2025 with **flat revenue** but **70% profit decline**, marking a transition year as the company integrated the strategic FIBI acquisition while facing significant cost pressures across operations.

## Business Line Analysis

### 1. Oil & Gas (Core Business) - ~91% of Revenue

**Status: Stable but Margin-Compressed**

- Revenue essentially flat at $2.19B (+0.5% YoY)
- **FIBI Acquisition** (June 2025): Acquired 100% of Fortuna International (Barbados) Inc. for ~$394M, gaining 24% indirect participating interest in Corridor block
- Oil & gas properties increased from $2.67B to $2.99B on balance sheet
- Production & lifting costs rose **12.7%** ($417M → $470M), pressuring margins
- Goodwill increased from $39M to $140M due to FIBI acquisition

### 2. Power Generation - High Growth Segment

**Status: Strong Expansion**

| Metric | FY 2025 | FY 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity Sales | $73.8M | $47.0M | **+56.8%** |
| Power Plant Lease | $10.9M | $10.0M | +9.0% |
| Concession Finance Income | $37.5M | $37.8M | -0.7% |
| **Total Power-Related** | **~$122M** | **~$95M** | **+28%** |

**Key Assets:**
- **ELB (Energi Listrik Batam)**: 106MW capacity, construction nearing completion
- **MRPR (Medco Ratch Power Riau)**: 275MW gas-fired plant, operational
- **MCG & MSBT**: Geothermal and solar projects under service concession arrangements

### 3. Services (Drilling & O&M)

**Status: Accelerating Growth**

- O&M Services: $38.6M (+32.5% YoY)
- Other Services: $24.8M (+70.1% YoY)
- Reflects expanding drilling services and technical consulting demand
- Supported by fleet of drilling rigs and marine vessels

### 4. Construction

**Status: Wind-Down Phase**

- Collapsed 79.2% ($81M → $17M)
- ELB CCPP construction project nearing completion
- Expected to remain minimal going forward

## Strategic Moves in 2025

1. **FIBI Acquisition** ($394M): Added Corridor block reserves, strengthening core O&G position
2. **Power Portfolio Expansion**: ELB and MRPR plants driving 56% revenue growth in electricity
3. **Share Buyback**: Aggressive treasury accumulation (~384M shares added)
4. **Services Diversification**: O&M and other services growing 30-70% annually

## Outlook & Risks

### Positives
- Power segment on strong growth trajectory (+57% electricity sales)
- FIBI acquisition adds reserve base and production capacity
- Services business showing momentum
- Share buyback supports per-share metrics

### Concerns
- **Margin compression**: Cost inflation outpacing revenue growth
- **Tax burden**: ~72% effective tax rate is unsustainable
- **Leverage**: Debt increased to fund acquisitions
- **Commodity exposure**: 91% revenue from oil & gas creates volatility

## Investment Thesis

**HOLD / ACCUMULATE ON WEAKNESS**

MEDC is in transition: the core O&G business remains a cash cow, but margin pressures are real. The power segment's 57% growth demonstrates successful diversification. The 70% profit drop is concerning but includes one-time items and reflects temporary cost inflation. The aggressive share buyback suggests management sees value at current levels.

**Key Monitor Points:**
1. Oil & gas production cost trends
2. Power segment margin expansion as ELB reaches full capacity
3. Tax rate normalization
4. FIBI/Corridor block contribution to production
5. Debt service coverage ratio
