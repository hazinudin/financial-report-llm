# PT Archi Indonesia Tbk (ARCI) — Q2 2026
---

*All figures are in USD millions unless stated otherwise. The analysis covers the unaudited interim consolidated financial statements for the **six months ended 30 June 2026** (H1 2026), with comparative prior-year data for the six months ended 30 June 2025. Period-end balance-sheet comparisons are versus 31 December 2025. The financial statements were authorized for issuance on 27 August 2026.*

## 1. Executive Summary

PT Archi Indonesia delivered a blow-out H1 2026, with revenue surging 67.5% YoY to $322.3M and net profit more than doubling to $81.5M (+134.1%). Gross margin expanded to 45.3% as revenue growth outpaced the 53.7% increase in cost of goods sold, generating $146.0M in gross profit (+87.8%). The result was driven by the gold-mining segment, which contributed 79% of external sales, while the trading and processing arm added a further $71.0M. Yet the headline numbers mask severe balance-sheet tightness: working capital collapsed to just $1.3M from $10.8M at December 2025, the current ratio is a razor-thin 1.01x, and the cash ratio is 0.14x. The group is effectively operating with no liquidity buffer.

The income statement carries two major unresolved anomalies. First, consolidated operating profit is not disclosed, preventing calculation of operating margin and interest coverage. Second, the tax line recorded in the fact sheet produces a computed effective tax rate of **–37.04%**; the sustainability of this tax outcome is unverified and represents a material risk to H2 earnings if rates normalise toward statutory levels. Net finance expense rose to $18.3M (interest expense $19.2M less interest income $0.9M), consuming 14% of pre-tax profit. Other income of $7.3M—driven by silver sales, FX gains, and miscellaneous items—is non-recurring in nature.

On the balance sheet, equity grew a healthy 14.0% to $413.6M, reducing the debt-to-equity ratio from 1.83x to 1.60x. However, total loans remain elevated at $486.6M (1.18x equity), and net debt cannot be computed because the fact sheet does not disclose securities held against debt. Cash fell $2.0M to $22.9M despite $92.1M of operating cash flow, because investing outflows ($58.8M) and financing obligations absorbed the remainder. Free cash flow was $72.4M, a sharp turnaround from prior periods, but the quality of earnings depends on whether the tax outcome and FX gains repeat.

No market data were provided, so valuation multiples and a target price cannot be calculated. Factoring in the explosive earnings growth, the precarious liquidity position, and the unhedged gold exposure, we maintain a **HOLD (High Risk)** stance. The stock is a pure proxy for spot gold with leverage, but the balance sheet is one working-capital swing away from stress.

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price

The fact sheet does not disclose gold production volume, grade, or average realised price for H1 2026. The static company profile notes a FY2026 production target of 155–168 koz and Q1 2026 output of 29 koz (+38% YoY), but H1 volume and unit cost data are absent. Without production disclosures, it is impossible to disaggregate the 67.5% revenue increase into volume, grade, or price effects. Revenue recognition is spot-based; the group has no forward sales or commodity hedging (Note 32d), so the top line moves one-for-one with realised gold prices. The IDR/USD exchange rate is a secondary factor: the group reports net foreign-currency liabilities of $38.9M and has no FX hedging policy, but because all H1 2026 sales were domestic, FX did not directly affect revenue translation.

### 2.3 Revenue Composition Observations

Customer concentration remains extreme. The top four customers accounted for **78.3%** of H1 2026 sales:

| Customer | H1 2026 Sales (USD M) | % of Revenue |
|----------|----------------------|--------------|
| PT Suka Jadi Logam | 86.3 | 26.8% |
| PT Swarnim Murni Mulia | 79.2 | 24.6% |
| PT Indo Prosperity International | 54.7 | 17.0% |
| PT Lotus Lingga Pratama | 32.4 | 10.1% |
| PT Untung Bersama Sejahtera | 0.8 | 0.3% |

PT Suka Jadi Logam was not a customer in H1 2025 or FY2024, while PT Untung Bersama Sejahtera collapsed from 26.0% of FY2024 sales to 0.3%. All H1 2026 revenue was domestic; export sales to Thailand, Singapore and the UAE that were present in FY2025 fell to zero in H1 2026. The rapid customer churn and domestic-only mix raise questions about offtake diversification and pricing power.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

Revenue sustainability is tied entirely to spot gold prices and domestic smelter appetite. The progressive Indonesian gold royalty (7%–16%) escalates with price, acting as a partial automatic stabiliser at high prices but compressing margins if prices fall. Seasonality suggests a Q3 volume peak, yet without disclosed mine sequencing or grade-control data, forward revenue visibility is limited. Management’s FY2026 production target implies a H2 acceleration, but this is planning guidance, not evidence.

## 10. Strengths

### 10.1 Financial Strengths
- H1 OCF of $92.1M and FCF of $72.4M represent a material improvement in cash generation.
- Equity base expanded 14.0%, deleveraging D/E from 1.83x to 1.60x.
- Pre-tax margin of 40.2% and net margin of 25.3% are robust on a global mining comparative basis.

### 10.2 Operational Strengths
- Domestic sales eliminate cross-border receivable risk; DSO is effectively nil.
- Long-dated mining licenses (to 2041, extendable) underpin asset life.
- 100% economic ownership of MSM and TTN (via 99.5% direct + 0.5% ARPTE) ensures full consolidation of core cash flows.

### 10.3 Strategic Strengths
- Refinancing in November 2025 pushed bank maturities to December 2030, removing near-term refinancing overhang.
- Trading/processing segment (EMAS) is scaling, adding a second revenue leg.
- Geothermal JV provides long-term optionality on energy-transition monetisation.

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks
- **HIGH — Liquidity crunch:** Current ratio 1.01x and working capital of only $1.3M (down from $10.8M at Dec 2025) leaves no buffer against operational delays or tax settlements.
- **HIGH — Tax rate anomaly:** The –37.04% effective tax rate is unexplained and likely unsustainable; normalisation to statutory rates (22% or CoW-tiered) would slash net profit by $20M+ on the same PBT.
- **MODERATE — Customer churn:** PT Suka Jadi Logam appeared as the largest customer with no prior-year track record; top-four concentration is 78.3%.

### 11.2 Structural Risks
- **HIGH — Leverage:** Loans/equity 1.18x and total liabilities/equity 1.60x remain elevated for a single-asset-class miner.
- **HIGH — Unhedged gold exposure:** No forward sales or hedging means every $100/oz move hits revenue directly; progressive royalty amplifies margin compression at lower prices.
- **MODERATE — Mine maturity:** Mining properties amortisation and reclamation provisions ($14.1M) rise over time; sustaining capital may exceed $20M annually.

### 11.3 Market and Regulatory Risks
- **MODERATE — Domestic market dependence:** Export revenue fell to zero in H1 2026; reliance on domestic smelters creates single-jurisdiction offtake risk.
- **MODERATE — Royalty escalation:** Progressive royalty accruals jumped to $7.6M (Note 17) from $4.4M at Dec 2025; effective royalty rate for H1 is unverified but trending higher.
- **LOW — Pillar 2 tax:** Group assessed as out of scope for Indonesia’s Pillar 2 minimum tax.

### 11.4 Valuation Concerns
Valuation multiples cannot be assessed due to missing market data. On fundamental grounds, the equity story is entirely leveraged to gold price and tax normalisation; any multiple expansion would be purely commodity-driven.
