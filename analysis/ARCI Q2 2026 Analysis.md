# PT Archi Indonesia Tbk (ARCI) — Q2 2026 (Six Months Ended June 30, 2026)

---

**Reporting basis.** All figures in **US dollars (USD)**, full units, from the consolidated interim financial statements of PT Archi Indonesia Tbk and subsidiaries for the six-month period ended **June 30, 2026** (comparative: six months ended June 30, 2025 for P&L/cash flow; December 31, 2025 for the balance sheet). The statements were authorized for issuance by the directors on **August 27, 2026**; the fact sheet flags the interim set as audited per its source metadata, though interims are customarily reviewed rather than fully audited — we treat the figures as canonical per the fact sheet throughout. Statements were prepared in connection with the company's bond-offering plan under OJK regulations (Note 37). Key share count: 25,235,000,000 shares issued and fully paid at June 30, 2026 (Dec 31, 2024: 24,835,000,000).

## 1. Executive Summary

ARCI delivered a step-change first half. Revenue rose **67.5% YoY to US$322.3M** (1H25: US$192.4M) on a double driver: volumes — Q2 gold sales of roughly 32,000 oz, +39% qoq, reflecting deliveries deferred out of a weak Q1 — and a materially higher realized gold price environment (Indonesia's HMA gold reference averaged ~US$4,746/toz in 2026 vs ~US$3,376/toz in 2025, per Kompas). Gross profit nearly doubled to **US$146.0M (+87.8%)**, gross margin expanded ~490bps to **45.3%**, and net profit attributable to owners more than doubled to **US$80.3M (EPS US$0.0032, +129%)**. Annualized ROE prints at an extraordinary **83.9%** — but that is the arithmetic of a gold-price super-cycle quarter, not a run-rate.

The balance sheet tells a more nuanced story. Equity grew 14.0% to US$413.6M on retained earnings, but leverage remains heavy: total debt-to-equity **1.60x**, loans-to-equity **1.18x**, and liquidity is razor-thin — current ratio **1.01x**, quick ratio **0.38x**, working capital just **US$1.3M**. Cash slipped to US$22.9M despite US$92.1M of operating cash flow because the company paid a US$30M final FY2025 dividend (June 9, 2026), US$67.5M of income taxes, and continued to service the new US$421M-equivalent CFA/MMQ loan stack at ~US$19.2M of half-year interest expense. Free cash flow was still strongly positive at **US$72.4M**.

The earnings mix is broadening: the 51%-owned downstream Trading and Gold Processing segment (EMAS/"Lotus Archi") contributed **US$70.95M of external 1H26 sales** (vs US$38.8M a year ago) and drove NCI profit up 263% to US$1.2M. All 1H26 revenue was domestic — export revenue (US$96.4M in FY2025) vanished — and the top four customers took 78.6% of sales, with new offtaker PT Suka Jadi Logam alone at 26.8%.

The forward setup is less flattering than the backward one. Gold ended 1H26 at ~US$4,007/toz after the worst quarterly drop in 13 years, and sell-side H2 2026 forecasts cluster at US$4,300–4,375/oz (Deutsche Bank, ING, Bernstein). The 1H price tailwind is fading; the FY2026 story must now be volume (FY2026 target 155–168 koz; UOB Kay Hian sees 33–36 koz sold in 3Q26 and FY26F net profit of ~US$204M) and the 4→6 Mtpa plant expansion (up to US$120M, internally funded). No market data was provided for this period, so no live P/E, P/B, EV or target multiple is quoted here. **Rating: HOLD (High Risk)** — leverage, liquidity, customer concentration and the gold-price reset cap the rerating case despite exceptional profitability.

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price

The 67.5% growth splits roughly into a volume leg and a price leg. On volume: Q1 2026 mine production was ~29 koz (+38% YoY) per [[ARCI Q1 2026 Analysis]], boosted by the Araren pit restart and Marawuwung start-up, and Q2 sales of ~32,000 oz (+39% qoq per UOB Kay Hian, Aug 31, 2026) captured deferred 1H deliveries — implying 1H26 gold sales on the order of 55–60 koz against 1H25 deliveries of roughly 40 koz implied by the comparative revenue base. On price: gold opened 2026 near US$4,313/oz, printed a record ~US$5,598/oz in January, and Indonesia's HMA reference averaged ~US$4,746/toz in 2026 vs ~US$3,376/toz in 2025 (Kompas, May 10, 2026). The company sells gold **entirely at spot, with no forward sales or commodity hedging** (Note 32d), so realized price follows the market with no cushion in either direction.

The arithmetic implies 1H26 realized pricing well above unit cost growth: COGS rose only 53.7% against 67.5% revenue growth. Within COGS, government royalty more than doubled to **US$41.8M from US$19.6M** (per the IDX-filed interim accounts, cited via the research brief) — the mechanical consequence of the PP 19/2025 progressive royalty scale (7–16%) that ratchets upward with the HMA reference price.

Top-four concentration was **78.60%** (1H25: 94.38%). The mix shifted violently: PT Suka Jadi Logam had zero sales in 1H25 and FY2024 and is now the largest customer; PT Untung Bersama Sejahtera collapsed from 25.96% of FY2024 revenue to 0.26%. All 1H26 revenue was **domestic** (Indonesia only); FY2025's US$96.4M of exports to Thailand/Singapore/UAE (Note 35c) went to zero in both 1H26 and 1H25 — a structural re-routing of refined output toward domestic buyers that remains an open verification item (see §2.4). Payment terms are 1–30 days post-delivery; trade receivables are consequently negligible (US$0.53M), so revenue is effectively cash-on-delivery.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

Three forces frame the second half. First, **price**: gold's 1H26 close of ~US$4,007/oz (-7% YTD, worst quarterly drop in 13 years per CNBC Indonesia, July 1, 2026) and bank forecasts of US$4,300–4,375/oz for H2 (Deutsche Bank/ING/Bernstein, June–July 2026) mean the YoY price uplift compresses toward zero by Q4. Second, **volume**: the FY2026 production target of 155–168 koz implies a heavy H2 load (~95–110 koz) versus ~55–60 koz sold in 1H — UOB Kay Hian models 33–36 koz in Q3 alone. Third, **policy**: the ESDM proposal to lift gold royalties to a 14–20% progressive scale was postponed in May 2026; the Danantara single-gate export regime was delayed to January 1, 2027 — limited direct impact while ARCI sells domestically, but royalty is computed on the higher of LBMA or realized prices under the scheme. A year-end JORC reserve update (3.9 Moz at 1.2 g/t end-2025; high-grade intercepts including 25.18 g/t over 3.1 m reported July 2026) is a potential catalyst. Watch items: whether export revenue returns, whether PT Suka Jadi Logam's share proves sticky, and Q3 realized price versus the ~US$4,746 HMA average.

### 4.2 Working Capital Components

Receivables are trivial by design — domestic terms of 1–30 days, cash-generative; DSO is **0.15 days** annualized. The real working-capital sink is **inventories at US$104.4M (+20.9% HoH)**: gold ore stockpiles (current) rose to US$66.1M from US$52.8M, with non-current stockpiles of US$79.2M at cost, and the obsolescence/NRV allowance sits at US$6.4M after a small recovery. DIO of **29.6 days** (annualized) understates the absolute commitment; stockpile build supports the H2 volume ramp. Other receivables jumped to US$32.5M — VAT receivables fell from US$22.4M to US$4.0M while "receivables from contractors for material used" spiked to US$5.6M from near zero (verification item). Taxes payable fell US$23.1M as US$67.5M of cash taxes settled the FY2025 liability plus 2026 instalments (Article 25 balance US$6.0M; Article 29 US$26.5M remaining).

NCI leakage remains small (1.5% of consolidated profit) but will scale with the downstream build-out — a modest structural dilution of owners' earnings, consistent with peers' JVs.

## 10. Strengths

### 10.1 Financial Strengths

- **Cash machine economics**: OCF +103% to US$92.1M, 113% cash conversion, DSO of 0.15 days, collections 101% of revenue — gold sold is gold collected.
- **Deleveraging direction**: D/E down from 1.83x to 1.60x, loans flat while equity compounds at 14% per half; covenant compliance confirmed across all three disclosure dates.
- **US$60.2M of unrecognized tax losses** (5-year usability) — a stored tax shield worth up to US$13.3M of retained earnings if recognized.
- Positive equity build: retained earnings +US$50.3M in six months even after the US$60M FY2025 payout program.

### 10.2 Operational Strengths

- **Cost curve position**: AISC down from US$2,224/oz to US$1,841/oz; at H2 2026 consensus gold of ~US$4,300/oz the margin is still ~US$2,400/oz before royalty/tax.
- Volume ramp de-risked: Araren restart, Marawuwung start-up, first Kopra underground ore; 1H26 already implies roughly 55–60 koz sold against a 155–168 koz target with H2 the seasonal peak.
- SG&A at 2.3% of revenue — best-in-class overhead intensity for the Indonesian mining complex.
- Aggressive exploration (244 holes / 70,138 m cumulative per the company's drill disclosures) with high-grade intercepts feeding a year-end JORC update.

### 10.3 Strategic Strengths

- **Downstream second leg emerging**: Trading & Processing revenue US$71.0M (+83%) with segment profit scaling 5x — the EMAS/Lotus Archi brand build adds a non-mine earnings stream and NCI only takes 1.5% of group profit.
- 4→6 Mtpa plant expansion funded internally — self-financed capacity growth of +30–40 koz/yr.
- CoW stability: MSM/TTN licenses to 2041, extendable 2×10 years; foreign divestment obligations fulfilled since 2016.
- Balance-sheet optionality in the Rp500bn bond subscription (carrying US$28.6M) and the Ormat geothermal JV.

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks

- **HIGH — Liquidity**: current ratio 1.01x, quick ratio 0.38x, working capital US$1.3M, unrestricted cash US$22.9M (largely pledged/limited insurance) against US$61.0M of current loan maturities and US$33.4M of taxes payable. Any delivery hiccup (plant, weather, offtake) transmits instantly to solvency of working capital.
- **HIGH — Gold price reset**: 1H26 closed at ~US$4,007/oz; H2 consensus US$4,300–4,375/oz and downside to US$3,800–4,400 if the Fed hikes (ING/DB/Bernstein). The +67.5% revenue print is backward-looking; Q4 2026 YoY comps face a much higher base.
- MODERATE — Q2-weighted delivery timing: 1H26 volume was flattered by deferrals from Q1; if H2 shipments revert to mine availability rather than backlog, the 155–168 koz target gets tight.
- MODERATE — Supplier-payment timing: the 25% drop in payments to suppliers against +54% COGS is a US$30M working-capital gift that reverses in H2.

### 11.2 Structural Risks

- **HIGH — Leverage & collateralization**: US$486.6M of loans against US$413.6M equity; essentially all assets pledged; cross-default on missed instalments or covenant ratios; Term SOFR floaters expose ~US$187k of pre-tax profit per 100bps (Note 32c).
- MODERATE — Royalty escalation: progressive PP 19/2025 scale (7–16%) doubles the royalty bill in gold up-moves (already US$41.8M in 1H26); the postponed 14–20% ESDM proposal would compress margins a further 300–600bps if revived. Under Danantara single-gate (Jan 2027), royalty is computed on the higher of LBMA or realized price.
- MODERATE — "Others" segment chronic drag (−US$4.4M 1H26, −US$5.0M FY2025) on US$190.4M of segment liabilities of unverified composition.
- LOW — FX: net foreign-currency liabilities of US$38.9M, no hedging policy; 10% IDR move = US$3.9M P&L effect.

### 11.3 Market and Regulatory Risks

- **HIGH — Customer concentration**: top four = 78.6% of revenue; the lead offtaker (PT Suka Jadi Logam, 26.8%) is a brand-new relationship with no track record in the comparatives.
- MODERATE — Single-gate export regime (delayed to Jan 1, 2027): ARCI currently sells only domestically, limiting direct impact, but the regime's pricing/USD-collection mechanics are untested.
- MODERATE — Governance/ownership: June 23, 2026 affiliate transaction moved 16.6bn shares (71.9% of the company) to PT Rajawali Kapital Emas at Rp1,100; ultimate control unchanged (Rajawali/Sondakh), free float unchanged at ~15%, but the transaction printed a deep-discount-to-nothing reference and the stock fell ~7% on the news.
- LOW — PSAK 118/119 adoption (Jan 2027): no material effect anticipated.

### 11.4 Valuation Concerns

- No live market data for this note — P/E, P/B and EV are **not disclosed**; any multiple framework rests on the Rp1,100 affiliate print, which is an intra-group transfer price, not a market-clearing one.
- The 83.9% annualized ROE is a cyclical peak; paying peak multiples for peak returns is the classic gold-equity trap (see [[Gold]] and [[Gold_July_2026_Update]]).
- Interest expense at 6% of revenue and CoW taxes at ~15% cap net margin well below the gross-margin headline regardless of gold direction.

### 13.1 Financial Health Score: 5/10

Profitability and cash generation are exceptional (OCF +103%, FCF US$72.4M), but leverage (1.60x D/E), zero working capital, pledged collateral across the entire asset base, and US$22.9M of unrestricted cash against a US$653.8M undiscounted contractual maturity ladder hold the score at the middle of the range.

### 13.2 Operational Performance Score: 9/10

Volume ramp executed (Q2 sales +39% qoq, Araren/Marawuwung/Kopra all contributing), AISC on a multi-year downtrend, DSO near zero, SG&A at 2.3% of revenue, and the downstream segment scaling. Deducted marginally for the reliance on deferred Q1 deliveries to make the 1H number.

### 13.3 Growth Score: 7/10

FY2026 production target 155–168 koz (+26–38% vs FY2025's 122 koz), 6 Mtpa plant by end-2027 (+30–40 koz/yr), drilling feeding a JORC update, downstream revenue compounding. The cap is external: growth is priced in USD in a market whose H2 consensus is US$4,300/oz.

### 13.4 Valuation Score: 6/10

On the Rp1,100 affiliate reference, annualized earnings multiples look mid-single-digit and P/B ~1.5x against a cyclical-peak ROE — statistically cheap, cyclically suspect. No live market data was provided; this score is conditional, not measured.

### 13.5 Risk Profile

**High risk.** Concentrated in three vectors: (i) gold price (100% spot exposure, no hedging, progressive royalty amplification); (ii) balance-sheet fragility (zero working capital, full collateralization, cross-defaults); (iii) single-offtaker/customer-mix churn. Mitigants: CoW license security to 2041+, covenant compliance, and a self-funding cost-down track record.

## 14. Investment Thesis

### 14.1 Bull Case

H2 2026 is ARCI's seasonal volume peak with 95–110 koz still to deliver against the 155–168 koz target, the 6 Mtpa expansion and Marawuwung underground add structural ounces by 2027, and the JORC update could add reserves at a time when the market is paying for gold-sector earnings momentum. Even at US$4,300/oz, AISC of ~US$1,841/oz leaves ~US$2,400/oz of operating margin; at any Rp1,100-ish price the equity trades at mid-single-digit annualized earnings multiples with a 3–4% dividend yield capacity. UOB Kay Hian's US$204M FY26F implies a further ~2.5x of 1H-owners' earnings in H2 on volume alone.

### 14.2 Bear Case

Gold's 13-year-worst quarter, H2 consensus of US$4,300–4,375/oz with downside to US$3,800, and a possible revival of the 14–20% royalty proposal compress the margin from both ends. The balance sheet has no shock absorber: US$1.3M working capital, 0.38x quick ratio, US$61M current maturities, everything pledged with cross-defaults. A 20% gold correction to ~US$3,200/oz would still be profitable per ounce, but would likely freeze the expansion, force payout cuts, and test covenant ratios — with an 83.65% controller and ~15% float, exit liquidity in stress is poor.

### 14.3 Key Catalysts

- Q3 2026 sales volume (street: 33–36 koz) and realized price vs the ~US$4,746 HMA 1H average.
- Year-end JORC reserve/resource update after the 244-hole drill program.
- Progress and cost discipline on the 4→6 Mtpa expansion (CIP currently US$45.4M).
- Danantara single-gate implementation (Jan 2027) — LBMA-benchmark mechanics and USD-collection terms.
- Recognition or resolution of the US$33.3M tax-refund claims and the US$60.2M tax-loss DTA.
- Any revival of the 14–20% royalty proposal post-deferment.

### 14.4 Recommendation

**HOLD (High Risk).** The operational story is the best in ARCI's listed history — costs down, volumes up, downstream scaling — but the 1H26 print is the top of the price cycle, the balance sheet offers no room for a gold correction, and valuation cannot be anchored without live market data (not disclosed for this note). Investors already positioned should hold for the H2 volume peak and the JORC catalyst with a tight review trigger; new money should wait for either (a) a material gold-price reset that the multiple already discounts, or (b) visible deleveraging (D/E below ~1.2x) that converts cyclical earnings into durable equity. **Review trigger: Q3 2026 results — if 3Q gold sales fall below ~30 koz or realized margin compresses more than 300bps qoq, downgrade to SELL; if the royalty proposal is shelved permanently and Q3 volumes print ≥35 koz, upgrade toward BUY.**
