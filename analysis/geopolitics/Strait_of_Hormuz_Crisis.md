## 3. The July 11, 2026 Incident — Detailed Reconstruction

### The Target: M/V GFS Galaxy

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Vessel** | M/V GFS Galaxy |
| **Flag** | Cyprus |
| **Type** | Container ship |
| **Location** | Transiting Strait of Hormuz |
| **Damage** | Engine room significantly damaged |
| **Casualties** | 1 crew member reported missing |
| **IRGC justification** | "Use of unauthorized route" |

### Immediate Aftermath

**Iran's declaration:** Following the strike, Iran declared the Strait of Hormuz closed until further notice. Per Windward: *"This appears to leave open the possibility that tolls could be imposed in the future."*

**US response:** CENTCOM stated the IRGC had "blatantly attacked" M/V GFS Galaxy. The third round of strikes hit ~140 Iranian military targets across the country.

**Iranian retaliation expansion:** Unlike prior phases where strikes were contained to Iran-US bilateral exchanges, the Jul 11 retaliation **geographically widened** to include:
- Prince Hassan Air Base in **Jordan**
- Air defense activations across **Qatar, Bahrain, and UAE**

This marks the first time the conflict has directly involved all four Gulf states in a single exchange.

**Qatar's unprecedented suspension:** The Qatar Transport Ministry's blanket maritime advisory is the first time a Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) state has issued a total maritime shutdown since the conflict began. It has direct implications for:
- Ras Laffan LNG exports (world's largest LNG facility)
- Qatalum aluminium smelter feedstock (already operating at ~65%)
- All Qatari fertilizer/ammonia/urea production dependent on Ras Laffan feed gas

### Qatar Maritime Suspension — Status

Per Marine Insight, Qatar's Transport Ministry restored port and shipping activities after a strategic **7-day suspension** (~Jul 11–18). However, the underlying **Ras Laffan feedstock constraint persists** — two Qatari LNG vessels were forced to abandon SoH crossing attempts after failing to secure Iranian transit approval. Iran has reportedly prioritized transit access for vessels linked to certain countries, adding a selective-access layer.

## 10. Scenario Analysis

> **Probability update (Jul 21, 2026):** Scenario A (Sustained Closure / Infrastructure War) probability has been **raised from ~35% to ~55%** — the Jul 14–21 escalation (10 strike nights, MOU void, nuclear sites hit, Bab el-Mandeb threat) has materially shifted the trajectory. Scenario B (Contested Transit) lowered from ~45% to ~30%. Scenario C (Rapid Diplomatic Breakthrough) lowered from ~20% to ~15%.

### Scenario A: Sustained Closure / Infrastructure War (Probability ~55% — RAISED)

**Assumptions:**
- SoH remains formally closed for 6+ months; US infrastructure strikes (power plants, bridges, refineries) continue
- US-Iran conflict escalates to sustained regional war; Bab el-Mandeb drawn in via Houthi "operational alliance"
- Qatar Ras Laffan partial restart by late Aug 2026 (Wood Mackenzie); full capacity 3–5 years out
- Oman mediation fails; Iraqi mediation track (Sep 30 US withdrawal) becomes primary
- Iran's new Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei remains isolated; Araghchi "upper hand" precondition unmet

**Implications:**
- Urea pushes toward NDSU "Extended Conflict" $996/st by Oct 2026 — **now base case**
- Global fertilizer prices spike; food security concerns emerge by Q4
- LNG shortage forces power rationing in Asia/Europe (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh already implementing)
- ESSA (Indonesia) and other non-Gulf producers capture massive price windfall
- Iranian domestic economy collapses (rial hyperinflation); regime change or desperate escalation likely

### Scenario B: Contested / Fragile Transit (Probability ~30% — LOWERED)

**Assumptions:**
- Oman/Iraq mediation produces partial ceasefire in Aug–Sep after both sides exhausted
- SoH reopens intermittently under toll/escort/military convoy regime
- Iran accepts limited nuclear verification in exchange for partial sanctions relief
- Qatar Ras Laffan partial LNG restart by late Aug 2026 (Wood Mackenzie); full capacity 3–5 years out

**Implications:**
- Urea oscillates $450–$650/st; ammonia $0.34–0.42/kg
- Insurance/freight premia remain structurally elevated
- "Quick reopening" price floor ($636/st) becomes the *upper bound* of down-legs
- Market volatility remains high but within a bounded range

### Scenario C: Rapid Diplomatic Breakthrough (Probability ~15% — LOWERED)

**Assumptions:**
- Iran accepts comprehensive nuclear curbs + verified sanctions sequencing under new supreme leader
- SoH reopens within 4–6 weeks under new international administration (UN/IMO mandate)
- Qatar repairs accelerate beyond 3–5 yr baseline
- US agrees to phased sanctions relief
- **Note: Jul 19 US strike on Darkhovin nuclear plant makes this scenario materially less likely**

**Implications:**
- Urea reverts toward $400–$450/st; ammonia $0.30–0.34/kg
- But Qatar Ras Laffan 3–5 yr repair means **structural floor persists** even in best case
- briefs.co long-run floor ($532/st) is the absolute minimum regardless of diplomacy
