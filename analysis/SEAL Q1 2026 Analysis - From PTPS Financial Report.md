## Key Observations

### Positive Indicators
- **Loss Reduction in Q1 2026:** Net loss of Rp 248M in Q1 2026 is significantly lower than the quarterly average of Rp 658M in 2025 (-62.4%)
- **Low Liability Ratio:** Liabilities represent only 4.9% of total assets
- **Stable Asset Base:** Total assets remained relatively stable with only 0.8% decline from Dec 2025

### Concerns
- **Continued Losses:** Company remains loss-making for consecutive years
- **Revenue Decline in Q1:** Q1 2026 revenue (Rp 551M) is lower than Q1 2025 run rate, possibly indicating seasonal factors or production issues
- **Asset Decline Trend:** Total assets have declined from Rp 38.42B (2024) to Rp 36.75B (Q1 2026)
- **Unpaid Investment:** PTPS still owes Rp 1.68B for share investment

## Conclusion

SEAL continues to operate as a palm oil company with PT Pulau Subur as a significant minority shareholder (30%). While the company showed strong revenue growth in 2025 compared to 2024 (+485%), it remains loss-making. However, Q1 2026 shows improvement with a significantly reduced net loss of Rp 248M compared to the 2025 quarterly average of Rp 658M.

The investment carrying value has gradually declined from Rp 9.39B (2024) to Rp 8.81B (Q1 2026) due to accumulated losses. The company maintains a healthy balance sheet with low liabilities (4.9% of assets).

For more detailed operational information (plantation location, hectare size, tree maturity), direct inquiry with the company or access to SEAL's own management reports would be necessary.
