# KAI (PT Kalimantan Aluminium Industry) - Investment Analysis

**Company:** PT Kalimantan Aluminium Industry ('KAI')  
**Sector:** Metal Processing (Aluminium Smelter)  
**Parent:** PT Alamtri Minerals Indonesia Tbk (ADMR) - 65% ownership via AIA  
**Status:** Pre-operational / Construction Phase  
**Location:** Kalimantan Industrial Park Indonesia (KIPI), North Kalimantan  

---

## Executive Summary

**KAI is ADMR's strategic transformation vehicle from coal pure-play to diversified aluminium producer.** As of Q1 2026, KAI has **not yet commenced commercial operations** and contributes **zero revenue** while consuming significant capital and generating losses.

## 8. Financial Projections (Speculative)

### 8.1 Breakeven Analysis

**Assumptions:**
- Smelter capacity: Not disclosed (industry standard: 500K-1M tonnes/year)
- Aluminium price: $2,400-2,800/tonne (LME 2025-2026 average)
- Operating margin: 15-25% (industry benchmark)
- Depreciation: $100-150M/year (based on $1.5B+ asset base)
- Interest expense: $60-80M/year (based on $969M debt @ 6-8%)

**Breakeven Revenue Required:** ~$800M-1.2B/year  
**Implied Production:** ~400K-500K tonnes/year at current prices

### 8.2 Impact on ADMR Consolidated (When Operational)

## 12. Conclusion & Recommendation

### 12.1 Investment Thesis Summary

| Aspect | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Strategic Logic** | ✅ Sound - diversification from coal to aluminium |
| **Execution Risk** | ⚠️ High - first-time aluminium producer, no timeline disclosed |
| **Financial Risk** | ⚠️ Medium-High - $969M debt with no revenue |
| **Upside Potential** | ✅ High - could double ADMR revenue at full capacity |
| **Downside Risk** | ⚠️ Medium - coal cash flows provide buffer |

### 12.2 Recommendation

**Status: WATCH / HOLD**

**Rationale:**
1. **Too early to assess** - No commercial operations, no revenue track record
2. **Coal provides buffer** - Strong coal cash flows ($46.6M OCF in Q1 2026) fund KAI development
3. **Monitor key milestones** - Commercial operation date, first production, offtake agreements
4. **Debt manageable for now** - DER 0.78x is elevated but sustainable with coal profits

**Key Questions for Management:**
1. What is the expected commercial operation date?
2. What is the total project cost estimate?
3. Are there signed offtake agreements?
4. What is the planned production capacity?
5. What are the energy sources/costs for the smelter?
