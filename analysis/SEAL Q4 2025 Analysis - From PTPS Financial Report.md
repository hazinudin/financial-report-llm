## Key Observations

### Positive Indicators
- **Revenue Growth:** Significant increase in revenue from Rp 436M to Rp 2.56B (+485%)
- **Reduced Losses:** Net loss decreased by 18.8% compared to 2024
- **Low Liability Ratio:** Liabilities represent only 5% of total assets

### Concerns
- **Continued Losses:** Company has been loss-making for consecutive years
- **Asset Decline:** Total assets decreased by 3.6%
- **Increased Liabilities:** Liabilities increased significantly (214%)
