## Executive Summary

ESSA operates Indonesia's largest privately-owned domestic LPG refinery and a major ammonia plant. The company reported **stable but slightly declining performance** in 2025 with significant balance sheet strengthening through complete debt elimination. However, **critical single-supplier dependency** remains the dominant investment risk.

**Key Investment Thesis:**
- ✅ Strong balance sheet (net cash, zero debt)
- ✅ Market-leading technology (KBR's world-first ammonia technology)
- ✅ Consistent profitability ($55M+ annual earnings)
- ✅ **US-Iran War tailwind:** Sole listed ammonia producer in Indonesia, positioned as "champion stock" by Meridian Research
- ✅ **Weak Rupiah benefit:** USDIDR at 17,300 boosts USD-denominated margins
- ⚠️ **CRITICAL:** 100% gas supply dependency on single supplier
- ⚠️ Declining revenue/margin trajectory (pre-war)
- ⚠️ Contract expiry cliff in 2027

## Business Model Overview

### Operations

**ESSA operates two main business lines:**

1. **LPG Refinery (Palembang, South Sumatra)**
   - Processes raw feed gas into LPG, condensate, and propane
   - Capacity: 70 MMSCF/day gas input
   - Largest privately-owned domestic LPG refinery in Indonesia

2. **Ammonia Plant (Banggai, Central Sulawesi - PAU)**
   - Operates ammonia production facility
   - Uses KBR's Reforming Exchanger System & Purifier Technology
   - **World's first application** of this technology
   - One of the biggest industrial projects in Eastern Indonesia
   - Contract capacity: 62 MMSCFD gas input

## Critical Investment Risks

### 🔴 CRITICAL: Single Supplier Dependency

**Auditor's Emphasis of Matter (Note 33):**

> "The Group buys **all of its raw feed gases solely** from a certain State Owned Enterprise and joint body of several oil and gas contractors, which is the **sole supplier available at the moment**. Disruption of supply of raw feed gas could cause a delay in manufacturing of LPG, condensate and ammonia and a possible cessation of sales, which would adversely affect operating results. Further, **termination of the aforementioned contracts could result in cessation of the business of the Group**."

**Gas Supply Details:**

| Plant | Supplier | Contract Volume | Contract Expiry |
|-------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **LPG (Palembang)** | Pertamina EP (State-Owned) | 70 MMSCF/day | Dec 31, 2027 |
| **Ammonia (Sulawesi)** | JOBPMTS (Pertamina Medco JV) | 62 MMSCFD | 2027 |

**Key Risk Factors:**
- ❌ **NO gas field ownership** - ESSA only has purchase contracts
- ❌ **100% dependency** on Indonesian domestic gas production
- ❌ **No alternative suppliers** available
- ❌ **No gas price hedging** instruments in place
- ❌ Contract expiry cliff in 2027 (both plants)

### Gas Pricing Structure

**NOT fixed-price contracts:**
- Long-term supply secured, but **prices are formula-based**
- Subject to periodic renegotiation (11+ amendments for LPG contract)
- Likely linked to Indonesian domestic gas pricing mechanisms
- **NOT directly indexed to global benchmarks** (Henry Hub, JKM, TTF)
- Company disclosure: *"Gas prices are very unstable... The Group might be negatively impacted by the increase in the gas price"*

**Risk Management:** Only operational efficiency (no financial hedging)

## Revenue Decline Analysis (2025 vs 2024)

### Root Causes

**2.1% revenue decline ($6.4M decrease) driven by:**

1. **Price Formula Changes**
   - **LPG**: Price formula amended November 2024 (effective August 2024) with Pertamina Patra Niaga
   - **Ammonia**: Price aligned to regional price index (January 2023 amendment with Genesis Corporation)
   - Likely lower realized prices in 2025

2. **Potential Volume Decline**
   - No explicit production volume disclosure
   - Ammonia revenue flat (↓0.3%) suggests stable operations
   - LPG revenue declined ↓13% ($5.4M decrease) - more significant concern

3. **Customer Concentration Impact**
   - **Pertamina-related parties**: 86.6% of revenue ($255.4M)
   - **Genesis Corporation** (Japan): 100% of ammonia offtake
   - Any customer reduction directly impacts revenue

### Ammonia Production Assessment

- ✅ Full year operations assumed

## US-Iran War Impact (Post-Q4 2025 Development)

**Meridian Research positions ESSA as their "champion stock"** — the sole listed ammonia producer in Indonesia, de-levered with best-in-class utilization rates, benefiting from enormous macroeconomic tailwinds.

### Supply Shock Dynamics
- **Strait of Hormuz closure** blocks ~30% of global urea/ammonia exports and 20% of global LNG trade
- **3.3M MT supply removed** from Saudi, Iran, and Qatar (top ammonia exporters)
- **Price surge:** Ammonia (ME) from $480/t to $700/t (+46%); Urea (ME) from $490/t to $907/t (+85%)
- **India emergency tender:** 2.5MT at $935-960/t vs. $510/t two months ago (+83%)
- **Urea-ammonia spread:** ~$200/t premium expected to mean-revert as ammonia catches up

### Rupiah Weakness as Tailwind
- USDIDR hit all-time high near 17,300 (smashing 1998 record of 16,745)
- ESSA reports in USD but has IDR-denominated costs — weak Rupiah is a **net positive** for margins
- Meridian Research is long USDIDR (average entry ~16,850), expects further depreciation

### Key Risk
- **Meridian's key risk:** "The opening of SoH (real opening)" — they don't see it happening soon
- If SoH reopens, ammonia prices could normalize from $700/t back toward $480/t (-30%+)

### Meridian's Summary
- **Long:** USD, ESSA
- **Short/Avoid:** JCI
- **Warning:** "Odds of winning in the coming type of market is paper-thin low. Trim your positions."

## Conclusion

**ESSA presents a high-risk, moderate-reward investment profile:**

**Strengths:**
- Technologically advanced operations
- Strong financial position post-deleveraging
- Consistent cash generation
- Strategic positioning in blue ammonia

**Critical Weakness:**
- **Single-supplier dependency is an existential risk** that cannot be overstated. The auditor's emphasis of matter on this issue is a major red flag that should discount any valuation significantly.

**Verdict:**
- **Speculative Hold** for existing investors with high risk tolerance
- **Avoid** for conservative investors
- **Key catalyst**: Contract renewal progress in 2026-2027 will be the primary value driver
- **Entry point**: Wait for clarity on gas supply contract extensions or significant valuation discount

**Final Note:** The single-supplier risk is so material that it overshadows all other positive factors. Investors should only consider ESSA if they have high conviction on contract renewal certainty and are adequately compensated for the binary risk profile.
