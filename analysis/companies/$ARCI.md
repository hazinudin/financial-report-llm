# ARCI — PT Archi Indonesia Tbk

**Sector:** Gold Mining
**Rating:** HOLD (High Risk)
**FY2025:** HOLD (Rerating Watch — Special Situation)

## Business

## Key Risks
- Razor-thin liquidity (CR 1.02x, Working Capital only $3.2M)
- High leverage (DER 1.70x, net debt matches equity)
- Progressive gold royalty (7%–16%) compresses margins above ~$4,000/oz
- New offtaker concentration (PT Suka Jadi Logam = 40.5% of sales)
- Gold price dependency; correction from $5,595 peak to ~$4,176
