# INDR — PT Indo-Rama Synthetics Tbk

**Sector:** Textile / Polyester / Minerals
**Rating:** Uninvestable at current margins

## Business
Four segments: Spun Yarns (loss), Polyester (loss, doubled), Minerals (only profitable segment, $5.6M), Others. Operating in Indonesia, Turkey, Uzbekistan. Gold mining segment in Cianjur, West Java.

## Key Financials (FY2025)

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2024 | YoY |
|--------|--------|--------|-----|
| Revenue | $778.8M | $877.7M | −11.3% |
| Gross Profit | $12.1M | $7.7M | +58.4% |
| Gross Margin | 1.6% | 0.9% | +70 bps |
| Net Loss | −$11.6M | −$13.8M | −15.9% |

## FY2021 → FY2025 Collapse
| Metric | FY2021 | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Revenue | $978.2M | $778.8M |
| Gross Margin | 14.7% | 1.6% |
| Net Profit | $84.6M | −$11.6M |

$96.2M swing from profit to loss driven entirely by gross margin collapse.

## Key Risks
- Structural margin compression from 14–15% to 1–2% era
- Fixed costs $59M/year (depreciation + G&A + interest) exceed gross profit
- Export markets declining
- Textile/polyester segments structurally loss-making
