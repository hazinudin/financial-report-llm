# TINS — PT Timah Tbk

**Sector:** Tin Mining & Processing (State-owned — MIND ID)
**Rating:** HOLD (Operational — pending valuation anchor)

## Business
State-owned integrated tin miner under MIND ID holding. Tin mining generates ~90% of revenue. MIND ID Trading Limited = ~48% of revenue. Coal and construction segments remain loss-making. Five IUPs temporarily suspended (4 extended in July 2026, 1 unresolved).

## Key Risks
- Single-customer concentration (MIND ID Trading = ~48% of revenue)
- IUP license suspensions create operational uncertainty
- PP 19/2025 progressive royalty (3–10%)
- Capex collapse (only Rp 88B H1 vs Rp 300B depreciation) — asset base erosion
- Tin price dependency (record run may not be sustained)
