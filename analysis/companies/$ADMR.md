# ADMR — PT Alamtri Minerals Indonesia Tbk

**Sector:** Coal Mining / Metal Processing (Aluminium diversification)
**Rating:** HOLD (Development Risk)

## Business
Pure coal miner transforming into diversified aluminium producer via 65%-owned KAI subsidiary (Kalimantan Aluminium Industry). KAI's first aluminium exports shipped June 2026 (35,063 MT to US and Korea). Phase 1 capacity 500K tons/year, ramping to 1.5M tons.

## Strategic Context
KAI carries $969M syndicated loan (SOFR+margin, matures May 2031). No principal repayments made yet. KAI contributed $0 revenue but consumed 53% of group assets and 86.7% of liabilities in Q1 2026. Heavy capex cycle — FCF deeply negative. Coal segment remains the sole cash generator.

## Key Risks
- KAI execution risk (first-time aluminium producer, no COD disclosed)
- Related-party concentration (AIS 36.6% of revenue)
- Deeply negative FCF during smelter ramp-up
- Interest on $969M debt becomes expensed upon commercial production
- DHE SDA forex controls
