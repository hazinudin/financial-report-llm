# KLBF — PT Kalbe Farma Tbk

**Sector:** Pharmaceuticals / Consumer Health
**Rating:** HOLD (Moderate Risk)

## Business
Indonesia's largest pharmaceutical company with 50+ subsidiaries. Four segments: Prescription Pharma, Consumer Health, Distribution & Logistics, and Nutritionals. Distribution dominates revenue (thin margin) while Prescription/Consumer Health drive high-margin gross profit. KGB biologics ownership increased to 78.59% ($63.75M). License commitments up to Rp 985B.
