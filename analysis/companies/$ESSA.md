# ESSA — PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk

**Sector:** Petrochemicals (Ammonia / LPG)
**Rating:** BUY — Target IDR 1,100–1,200

## Business
Largest private ammonia producer in Indonesia. 700K MT/year nameplate capacity (KBR Purifier Plus technology). SE Asia reference price basis (~$750/MT at Q2 peak vs ~$360/MT NE Asia spot). Genesis Corporation (Japan) offtake linked to regional index. Blue ammonia feasibility study underway ($120–150M capex, end-2026). SAF pre-feasibility near completion.

## Strategic Catalysts
- US-Iran war supply shock: ammonia ME from $480/t to $700/t (+46%)
- May 2026 35–40 day plant turnaround → +9% post-turnaround production uplift
- Blue ammonia MoU with JOGMEC/Mitsubishi/ITB
- Debt-free fortress balance sheet + windfall cash flow funds all capex

## Key Risks
- 100% gas supply on single supplier (Pertamina), contracts expire Dec 2027
- Commodity price cyclicality (Strait of Hormuz reopening key risk)
- NCI in PAU subsidiary = 30% (owners capture only 70% of ammonia profits)
- Market may treat windfall as "peak earnings"
