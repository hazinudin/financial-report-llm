# PTPS — PT Pulau Subur Tbk

**Sector:** Palm Oil Plantation (Small-scale FFB)
**Rating:** HOLD (High Risk)

## Business
Micro-scale palm oil FFB producer. Pure-play South Sumatra (Palembang, 1,090 Ha planted). 91–100% of sales to single customer PT Gelumbang Agro S. 30% stake in SEAL associate. Rp 42.6B CPO mill construction commitment (delayed past May 2025 target).

## Key Risks
- Single-customer dependency (PT Gelumbang Agro S = 91–100% of sales)
- Tiny scale with no geographic diversification — most exposed to South Sumatra weather
- El Niño yield cliff expected H1 2027 (9–12 month lag from May 2026 onset)
- DSI/Permendag 16/2026 SOE export regime from Jan 2027 compresses producer margins
- CPO mill construction uncertain
- B50 demand pull benefits CPO price but SOE channel captures export margin
