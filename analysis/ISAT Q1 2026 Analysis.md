# PT Indosat Tbk (ISAT) — Q1 2026
---
[[ISAT Q1 2026 Analysis]]

## 1. Executive Summary

All figures in this note are stated in millions of Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and are sourced from the unaudited interim financial statements for the three months ended 31 March 2026, unless otherwise noted. Income-statement and cash-flow comparisons are against the three months ended 31 March 2025; balance-sheet comparisons are against 31 December 2025. ISAT reported revenue of IDR 15,220,661 million, up 12.10% year-on-year per the deterministic growth ratio. Operating profit rose 8.19% to IDR 3,018,146 million, while net profit increased 8.03% to IDR 1,525,640 million. The more consequential figure for equity holders is net profit attributable to owners, which jumped 13.75% to IDR 1,491,337 million, driving basic EPS to IDR 46.24 from IDR 40.65. That outperformance is entirely attributable to a 66% collapse in non-controlling interest (NCI) profit to IDR 34,303 million; whether this reflects an ownership restructuring, a cyclical subsidiary loss, or a prior-period one-off remains unresolved in the provided disclosures.

The balance sheet remains highly leveraged. Total assets grew 2.93% to IDR 122,101,965 million, equity expanded 3.87% to IDR 41,038,262 million, and total liabilities stand at IDR 81,063,703 million, producing a debt-to-equity ratio of 1.98x. Cash ended the period at IDR 5,564,426 million, supported by operating cash flow of IDR 6,525,266 million. However, working capital is deeply negative at IDR -14,370,117 million and the current ratio is 0.56x, underscoring structural reliance on short-term refinancing and stable vendor payables. Capital allocation is tilted toward network expansion—capex commitments with Huawei, Nokia, and Ericsson total IDR 4,752,577 million—and accelerated debt repayment of IDR 2,999,950 million.

No market data were furnished in the research stub, so valuation multiples, market capitalization, and a target price range are not disclosed. The equity exhibits a split personality: a cash-generative cellular core growing at double digits and deleveraging aggressively, offset by a balance sheet carrying IDR 40.8 trillion in lease liabilities, near-term loan and bond maturities in the second half of 2026, and earnings-quality questions around tax credits and NCI attribution. We assign a HOLD (Fundamental) rating pending resolution of these anomalies in the Q2 disclosure cycle.

## 10. Strengths

### 10.1 Financial Strengths
- Operating cash flow of IDR 6.5 trillion and a cash balance of IDR 5.6 trillion provide liquidity to service near-term maturities.
- Collections ratio of 99.2% indicates high-quality revenue cash capture.
- Equity base grew 3.87% in the quarter, absorbing heavy capex and debt repayment.

### 10.2 Operational Strengths
- Double-digit revenue growth in a mature cellular market suggests pricing power or data-volume expansion.
- MIDI segment growing at a rapid clip, diversifying away from pure cellular dependency.
- Network infrastructure scale—PPE and intangibles exceeding IDR 97 trillion—creates a wide moat against new entrants.

### 10.3 Strategic Strengths
- Portfolio of digital adjacencies: data center (SMT), electronic payments (APE, Nusacita), AI (CDG), and cloud (Google).
- Tower and fiber monetization optionality via held-for-sale assets and ToPA lease commitments (IDR 354,980 million received).
- Strong vendor relationships (Huawei, Nokia, Ericsson) securing equipment supply.

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks
- **HIGH — NCI Profit Collapse**: The 66% drop in NCI profit is unexplained. If cyclical, owners' EPS will reverse; if structural, comparables are broken. Lintasarta's swing to loss is a likely contributor.
- **MODERATE — Tax Credit Uncertainty**: The effective tax rate benefits from claims for refunds (IDR 174,553 million) and a low tax assessment impact. A normalization toward the statutory rate would reduce net margin.
- **MODERATE — Near-Term Maturities**: UOB short-term facility of IDR 1,204,045 million matures 26 August 2026; PUB I Tahap IV Seri E bond of IDR 201,000 million matures 2 September 2026. Refinancing risk exists.

### 11.2 Structural Risks
- **HIGH — Leverage**: D/E of 1.98x and total liabilities of IDR 81 trillion against equity of IDR 41 trillion leave limited buffer. Lease liabilities alone are IDR 40.8 trillion carrying value (fair value IDR 42.3 trillion).
- **MODERATE — Negative Working Capital**: Current ratio of 0.56x and working capital deficit of IDR 14.4 trillion imply reliance on short-term refinancing or stable payables management.
