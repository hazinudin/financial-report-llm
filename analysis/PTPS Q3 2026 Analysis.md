# PT Pulau Subur Tbk (PTPS) — Q3 2026
---

**Reporting basis.** All figures below are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR), drawn from the company's unaudited interim financial statements for the nine months ended 30 September 2026 (cumulative 9M basis), completed and approved by the Board on 27 October 2026 (per Note 1 of the source). The income-statement and cash-flow comparatives are the nine months ended 30 September 2025; the balance-sheet comparative is 31 December 2025. Values are in billions of IDR (Rp B) unless stated; per-share figures in Rp. Statements are interim and unaudited. The fact sheet flags several docling conversion misalignments (bank balances in Note 4, tax-revenue truncation in Note 15c, operating-expense column headers in Note 24, fair-value table totals in Note 27); these are flagged where material. No market data (price, market cap, shares-traded multiples) were provided for this analysis, so all market-based valuation ratios are shown as **not provided** rather than estimated.

## 1. Executive Summary

PT Pulau Subur Tbk delivered a striking operational reversal in Q3 2026. On a cumulative nine-month basis, revenue rose +13.86% YoY to Rp 58.12B (from Rp 51.05B in 9M 2025), gross profit rose +16.10% to Rp 33.17B (gross margin 57.07%), and net profit rose +7.74% to Rp 25.60B (net margin 44.05%), with basic EPS of Rp 11.81 vs Rp 10.96 (+7.76%). The cumulative numbers, however, understate the quarter: H1 2026 revenue was Rp 31.18B with a 49.77% gross margin ([[PTPS Q2 2026 Analysis]]), which implies a Q3 2026 standalone quarter of roughly Rp 26.9B revenue and an implied standalone gross margin near 65% — an exceptional sequential recovery off a depressed Q2, and an implied ~+80% YoY standalone revenue jump against a flat-to-weak national production backdrop (GAPKI/press projections had Q3 2026 Indonesian output falling on ENSO/IOD dryness — Kompas, 16 Jul 2026).

The external driver appears to be price rather than industry volume. South Sumatra FFB (TBS) reference prices rose through Q3 2026 — Rp 3,827.89/kg for period II July (+3.3% m/m) and Rp 3,891.68/kg for period I August 2026 (+1.67% vs July II), roughly +5% across July–August and ~+12% YoY vs the August 2025 reference of Rp 3,463/kg (South Sumatra Disbun price series via Bisnis.com/Tribun, Jul–Aug 2026). The national CPO reference price (HR CPO) was flat-to-firmer in Q3 (USD 1,000.90/MT July; USD 996.52/MT August; USD 1,007.51/MT September — Kemendag via Antara/Kompas, Sep 2026), sitting below the Q2 peak but recovering from the Q2 trough. The correct framing is a **Q2-trough → H2-recovery price path**, not a Q3 price spike: the provincial TBS reference price — the closest proxy for PTPS's realized selling price as a pure-play FFB seller — firmed ~5% sequentially and ~12% YoY, while company-specific volume (17,064 MT harvested in 9M 2026 per Note 8, at an annualized pace above H1's 9,225.8 MT) benefited from the normal August–October crop peak.

The balance sheet remains an outlier in the small-cap plantation universe: Rp 85.34B of cash (41.9% of total assets), a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.037x, loans-to-equity of 0.0005x, and an equity ratio of 96.4%. Working capital is effectively instantaneous — DSO 5.9 days, DIO 5.6 days, DPO 2.5 days, cash conversion cycle 9.0 days — reflecting an FFB-to-mill model where product is sold fresh and cashed within days (all outstanding trade receivables were received in full on 7 October 2026 per the subsequent-event note). Operating cash flow of Rp 32.21B (+37.49% YoY) against capex of only Rp 1.44B produced free cash flow of Rp 30.77B (FCF margin 53% of revenue). The FY2025 final dividend of Rp 9.75B was paid on 11 June 2026 — in Q2, not Q3 — so the Q3 cash build of ~Rp 20.1B is almost entirely retained operating cash flow (IDX announcement 0019/PTPS/IDXNET/V/2026).

The unresolved question is capital deployment. The Rp 42.57B CPO mill construction commitment with PT Sejahtera Jaya Abadi (Agreement No. 002/PS-SJA/III/2024, implementation period 1 April 2024–31 May 2025 — now lapsed) remains the single largest claim on the cash pile, yet 9M capex was a de minimis Rp 0.24B of PPE additions plus Rp 1.2B classified as other capex. CIP machinery stands at Rp 32.41B, 83.49% complete, with estimated completion in 2026, and no borrowing costs capitalized. Whether the mill completes, is re-scoped, or the cash is returned to shareholders is the central catalyst question. Post-period, South Sumatra karhutla (forest/land fires — 2,676 incidents to 10 September 2026 per BPBD) prompted an IDX explanation request to PTPS (letter S-13073/BEI.PP3/10-2026, answered 20 October 2026 by corporate secretary Liawan Kristianto), a read-across risk to Q4 volumes and to the sustainability of the Q3 margin peak.

Rating: **HOLD (High Risk)**. The quarter was excellent and the balance sheet is fortress-grade, but the earnings engine is a 1,090 Ha single-estate, single-customer FFB operation (PT Gelumbang Agro S. took Rp 53.46B of Rp 58.12B, ~92% of sales, per Note 22) levered to a provincial reference price PTPS does not control, with an El Niño yield cliff expected in H1 2027, a lapsed mill contract, and no market data provided to judge the price at which any of this is being bought.

## 2. Revenue Analysis

### 2.1 Revenue Performance

| Item | 9M 2026 (Rp) | 9M 2025 (Rp) | Change |

Revenue growth of +13.86% on a cumulative basis reverses the H1 2026 decline of −13.9% ([[PTPS Q2 2026 Analysis]]). The arithmetic implies an extraordinarily strong standalone Q3: 9M revenue Rp 58.12B less H1 Rp 31.18B gives ~Rp 26.94B for Q3 2026, versus a prior-year Q3 standalone of ~Rp 14.84B (9M 2025 Rp 51.05B less H1 2025 Rp 36.21B) — an implied ~+81% YoY. This is company-specific: national Q3 2026 palm oil production was projected to *decline* on ENSO/IOD weather pressure (Kompas, 16 Jul 2026; GAPKI full-year projection 56–58M MT with a 2027 decline on El Niño, Bisnis, 6 Oct 2026). The exact tonnage/ASP split for Q3 standalone is **not disclosed** — an explicit open question (see §2.2).

There is no foreign-exchange tailwind to speak of — sales are domestic Rupiah transactions to a mill in Muara Enim, and the vehicle lease is Rupiah-denominated at 5%.

External research identifies the counterparty as a CPO mill (KBLI 10431) at Desa Gelumbang, Muara Enim, on the Palembang–Prabumulih corridor (BPS Direktori Perusahaan Industri Provinsi Sumsel 2025); no public record of its ownership, affiliation with PTPS/PT Sekawan Kontrindo/mill contractor SJA, or of any 2026 offtake repricing/renewal was found. Whether the concentration and terms changed in 2026 is an open question. Single-segment: 100% of external sales are palm oil (FFB); no internal sales.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

Watch items: (1) **Post-period karhutla** — BPBD Sumsel recorded 2,676 fire incidents to 10 September 2026 with 15 districts in red-zone status; a palm-estate fire in Gunung Megang, Muara Enim, was extinguished mid-September; BNPB identified 44 burnt conversion/plantation areas in Sumsel by 19 October 2026. IDX queried PTPS directly (S-13073/BEI.PP3/10-2026); the company's 20 October 2026 reply content is not captured — whether PTPS's own Banyuasin/OKI estate blocks are affected is unknown, and this is the key read-across risk to Q4 2026 volumes. (2) **El Niño yield cliff** — NOAA put an 81% probability on a very strong El Niño in Oct–Dec 2026 (CNBC Indonesia, 30 Aug 2026); GAPKI projects a 2027 production decline; the profile flags an H1 2027 yield cliff for PTPS given the 9–12-month lag from a May 2026 onset. (3) **Policy/export regime** — Indonesia raised CPO export levies to 12.5% from 1 March 2026 and scrapped B50 for 2026 (Reuters, 14 Jan 2026); this affects mill economics and hence FFB bid prices downstream of PTPS. (4) **Pricing governance** — the tightened provincial enforcement of the TBS reference price (May 2026) is a modest structural support to realized prices. (5) No reserve/runway disclosures beyond 1,090.64 Ha planted (unaudited, Note 11) on HGU land rights maturing 2044–2045, which management believes renewable.

COGS composition (Note 23) shifted materially: repairs and maintenance jumped to Rp 4.94B from Rp 0.58B (estate upkeep catch-up — consistent with a heavier crop), fertilizer and chemicals fell to Rp 5.60B from Rp 8.46B (cost discipline and destocked fertilizer inventory), Land and Building Tax of Rp 0.42B was newly booked, and rent fell to Rp 0.012B from Rp 0.12B. ### 4.2 Working Capital Components

## 7. Segment and Subsidiary Analysis

### 7.1 Subsidiary Overview

PTPS has **no subsidiaries**. The only equity-accounted investment is the 30% associate PT Sumber Enim Alam Lestari (SEAL). Segment disclosure is a single segment: palm oil (FFB), external sales Rp 58.12B; segment gross profit, net profit, and assets are not disclosed.

### 7.2 The Core Operating Business

The crown jewel is the 1,090.64 Ha planted oil-palm estate (unaudited, Note 11) on HGU/permit land in South Sumatra, producing FFB sold fresh to mills — overwhelmingly PT Gelumbang Agro S. (~92% of 9M 2026 sales). Margins in 9M 2026: 57.07% gross, 44.05% net — remarkable for an FFB seller and a function of (a) mature, fully-amortized bearer plants (mature-plantation amortization in COGS), (b) zero financial leverage, (c) the recovering Sumsel TBS reference price, and (d) the crop-peak quarter. Bearer plants are insured for Rp 94.19B (PT Asuransi Sinar Mas); fixed assets/inventories insured Rp 5.8B. Land rights run to 2044–2045. Employee count: 27 permanent (vs 28 at Dec 2025).

### 7.3 Strategic Question Marks

1. **The Rp 42.57B CPO mill (PT Sejahtera Jaya Abadi, Agreement 002/PS-SJA/III/2024)** — implementation window 1 April 2024 to 31 May 2025 has lapsed; 9M capex Rp 0.24B is inconsistent with meaningful construction spend; CIP machinery Rp 32.41B at 83.49% complete with "estimated completion 2026"; no borrowing costs capitalized. Whether the contract is being executed, re-scoped, or effectively dormant — and what happens to the Rp 42.57B commitment — is the largest unresolved strategic question. External searches found no public 2026 announcement on progress, cancellation, or refund (IPO prospectus had earmarked ~50% of the ~Rp 92.7B IPO proceeds for this 10 t/hr PKS at Desa Gelebak Dalam, Banyuasin).
2. **SEAL associate** — 30% stake, investment balance Rp 8.80B (down from Rp 8.88B on a Rp 83.0M share of net loss); SEAL remains loss-making (unaudited 9M 2026: revenue Rp 2.63B, net loss Rp 276.7M; audited FY2025 net loss Rp 2.63B on Rp 37.05B assets) despite trees "entering productive age." PTPS still owes Rp 1.68B for shares not yet paid — a related-party payable to monitor.
3. **Trade payable settlement** — PT Anugerah Mustika Ostindo's Rp 2.09B balance at Dec 2025 dropped to nil; settlement verification is flagged in the source notes.

### 7.4 Growth Options

The mill is the only credible second leg: owning a 10 t/hr PKS would convert PTPS from a price-taking FFB seller (capturing none of the CPO processing margin) into a CPO producer — the strategic logic of the IPO. Until it is funded and completed, growth depends on estate maturity profile and TBS pricing. The HGU processing advance (Rp 1.2B) and the Rp 3.90B plantation-road maintenance accrual suggest estate-intensity investment rather than capacity expansion.

### 7.5 Intercompany and Related-Party Dynamics

Related-party items (Note 23/24): office rent to PT Sekawan Kontrindo (78.54% parent; ultimate shareholder Mr. Abunawar) Rp 41.25M (9M 2026) vs Rp 39.0M; vehicle/heavy-equipment rent to Sekawan Kontrindo Rp 0 vs Rp 119.6M (terminated); the Rp 1.68B unpaid-share payable to SEAL; SEAL equity-method loss Rp 83.0M. Key-management remuneration: Commissioners Rp 365.6M, Directors Rp 376.7M (9M 2026) — modest and flat YoY. Items to monitor: the customer-concentration related-party question (is PT Gelumbang Agro Sentosa affiliated?), the unpaid SEAL shares, and any mill-contractor (SJA) related-party links — all unresolved in the disclosures and external research.

### 10.2 Operational Strengths
- Implied Q3 2026 standalone gross margin ~65.5% and ~+81% YoY standalone revenue — best quarter in the company's listed history, on crop peak plus recovering Sumsel TBS reference pricing (~+5% sequential, ~+12% YoY Jul–Aug 2026, external).

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks
- **HIGH — Customer concentration**: PT Gelumbang Agro S. = ~92% of 9M 2026 sales (Rp 53.46B of Rp 58.12B), up from ~83% a year earlier; PT Arsi Griya's purchases collapsed from Rp 8.71B to Rp 0.36B, increasing dependence on one mill. Any offtake interruption or repricing is an existential revenue event. Affiliation/ownership of the counterparty is undisclosed.
- **HIGH — Karhutla / Q4 volumes**: 2,676 fire incidents in Sumsel to 10 Sep 2026 (BPBD), 15 districts red-zone, Muara Enim estate fires; IDX formally queried PTPS (answered 20 Oct 2026; content not captured). El Niño at 81% probability for Oct–Dec 2026 (NOAA) threatens the H1 2027 yield cliff (9–12-month lag).
- **MODERATE — Tax normalization drag**: ETR 15.95% → 19.03%; SP2DK for FY2024/2023 outstanding since 4 Aug 2026; Rp 2.27B prepaid tax still under Tax Office discussion.

### 11.2 Structural Risks
- **HIGH — El Niño yield cliff H1 2027** (profile flag; GAPKI projects 2027 national production decline): a repeat of the FY2025 pattern (revenue +11% but net profit −33% on a Rp 4.46B biological-asset loss) is plausible.
- **HIGH — Lapsed mill contract**: the SJA implementation window ended 31 May 2025 with no public update; Rp 42.57B committed against Rp 0.24B of PPE capex — stranded-commitment and execution risk.
- **MODERATE — Single estate, single region**: 1,090 Ha in South Sumatra; the most weather-exposed footprint possible; 27 permanent employees is a thin operational bench.

### 11.3 Market and Regulatory Risks
- **MODERATE — Export/regime risk**: Indonesia raised CPO export levies to 12.5% from 1 March 2026 and scrapped B50 for 2026 (Reuters, 14 Jan 2026); the profile flags the DSI/SOE export regime (Permendag 16/2026) from Jan 2027 compressing producer margins — affects mill buyers' ability to pay for FFB, transmitting to PTPS's realized price.
- **LOW/MODERATE — Level-3 biological-asset fair value**: Rp 2.07B gain in 2026 vs Rp 4.46B loss in 2025 swings other income; valuation-assumption risk in both directions.
- **LOW — Data-quality flags**: multiple docling misalignments in the source (bank balances, tax-revenue truncation, Note 24/27 headers) — immaterial individually, but a caution on precision.

### 11.4 Valuation Concerns
- **No market data provided** — the equity cannot be priced in this analysis; the risk is asymmetric information: a 43%-of-assets cash pile at near-zero yields (~0.55% blended interest income) erodes value if the mill never completes and payouts stay at ~50%.
- Quality-of-earnings caveat: the ~65.5% implied Q3 standalone gross margin is a crop-peak + price-recovery coincidence; normalizing to H1-like conditions (~50% GM) would cut annualized EPS materially. Any valuation anchored on Q3 run-rates overstates earnings power.

## 14. Investment Thesis

### 14.1 Bull Case
- Q3 2026 proves the estate can generate ~65% gross margins at the crop peak with recovering TBS pricing; annualized EPS ~Rp 15.75 against BVPS Rp 90.71 implies deep value if any reasonable multiple applies.
- Rp 85.34B cash (Rp 39.4/share) with zero debt gives multi-year downside protection and a fully-funded mill option: if the 10 t/hr PKS completes, PTPS captures CPO processing margin and diversifies away from FFB price-taking.
- Dividend capacity is trivially sustainable; a shift to a higher payout turns the cash drag into yield.
- SEAL's trees entering productive age offer a second, optioned earnings leg.

### 14.2 Bear Case
- One customer, one estate, one commodity, one province: the ~92% concentration into PT Gelumbang Agro S. means a single contract event resets revenue to near zero.
- Q3 is the cycle peak: El Niño (81% probability Oct–Dec 2026) drives an H1 2027 yield cliff; the FY2025 template (revenue up, net profit −33% on biological-asset losses) could repeat.
- The mill contract lapsed with no visible spend — if it never completes, Rp 85B of cash earning ~0.55% becomes a slow value trap, and the karhutla/IDX scrutiny hints at unpriced operational risk.
- ETR normalization to ~19% is already a realized headwind; further tax-office findings (SP2DK pending) could add.

### 14.3 Key Catalysts
1. Resolution of the Rp 42.57B SJA mill contract — completion, re-scope, cancellation, or refund (highest impact).
2. Q4 2026/full-year volume disclosure and karhutla impact on estate blocks (content of the 20 Oct 2026 IDX reply not yet public).
3. 2027 dividend policy given Rp 30.77B 9M FCF and no debt.
4. TBS reference-price trajectory into 2027 (B50/export-levy regime; SOE channel effects from Jan 2027).
5. SEAL turning profitable as its estate matures.
6. Resolution of the FY2024/2023 SP2DK and the Rp 2.27B prepaid-tax discussion.

### 14.4 Recommendation

**HOLD (High Risk).** The quarter was the best in PTPS's listed history and the balance sheet is unimpeachable, but the investment case is binary: an ~92%-single-customer FFB seller at a cycle-peak margin, facing an El Niño yield cliff in H1 2027, with the transformative mill option unresolved for 18 months past its contractual window and no market data provided to judge the entry price. **Target price range: not determinable without market data** — reference anchors are BVPS Rp 90.71 (of which ~Rp 39.40/share is cash) and normalized (H1-like, ~50% GM) annualized earnings power of roughly Rp 10–12/share, versus cycle-peak annualized EPS of ~Rp 15.75. Review trigger: any disclosure resolving the SJA mill contract (execution, cancellation, or refund), the karhutla impact on Q4 volumes, a change in the PT Gelumbang Agro S. offtake terms or ownership, or publication of market data permitting a priced recommendation.
