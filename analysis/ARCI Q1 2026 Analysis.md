# ARCI Tbk (ARCI) — Q1 2026
---

*All figures are in United States Dollars, full units, derived from the unaudited interim financial statements for the three months ended 31 March 2026. Income statement and cash flow comparatives are for the three months ended 31 March 2025; balance sheet comparatives are as at 31 December 2025.*

## 1. Executive Summary

ARCI delivered a step-change quarter in Q1 2026, with revenue surging 50.8% year-over-year to $136.9M and net profit nearly tripling to $30.2M. Basic EPS doubled to $0.0012, driven by a higher realized gold price and production of approximately 29 kilo ounces from MSM and TTN. As noted in [[ARCI Gold Production Seasonality & 2026 Forecast]], ARCI’s earnings are overwhelmingly gold-price driven rather than volume-driven; the 50.8% revenue jump reinforces that leverage.

Profitability margins expanded dramatically. Gross margin reached 42.8% and operating margin hit 40.8%, up from prior-year levels implied by much lower absolute gross and operating profits. However, interest expense remained a heavy drag at $9.0M, consuming roughly 16% of operating profit and keeping pre-tax margin at 35.0%. The effective tax rate was 36.9%.

The balance sheet grew 3.4% to $1.06B in total assets, while equity rose 8.3% to $392.9M. Liquidity is razor-thin: working capital is just $3.2M and the current ratio is 1.02x. Cash improved to $39.5M from $24.9M at year-end, supported by $48.7M in operating cash flow. Net debt stands at $397.1M, giving a net debt-to-equity ratio of 1.01x.

No market data was provided, so valuation multiples cannot be computed. With approximately 24.8 billion shares outstanding and book value per share of $0.0158, the equity structure is highly fragmented. The absence of a market cap or share price prevents a formal valuation anchor.

We rate ARCI a **HOLD (High Risk)**. The operational momentum is genuine, but the combination of elevated leverage, thin liquidity, and commodity-price dependency creates a binary risk/reward profile. A target price range cannot be established without market data; the key review trigger is the Q2 2026 production report and any covenant compliance disclosure.

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks
- **HIGH — Liquidity Strain:** Working capital of $3.2M and a current ratio of 1.02x leave no margin for error. Taxes payable increased significantly in three months, implying a large near-term cash outflow.
- **HIGH — Interest Burden:** Interest expense of $9.0M in the quarter absorbs roughly 16% of operating profit. Interest coverage is 6.2x, but any EBITDA contraction would quickly stress this.
- **MODERATE — Customer Concentration:** The top customer (PT Suka Jadi Logam) accounted for 40.5% of Q1 sales and is a new relationship with no track record.

### 11.2 Structural Risks
- **HIGH — Leverage:** Net debt of $397.1M essentially matches total equity. D/E is 1.70x and loans/equity is 1.19x. The balance sheet is highly geared to gold prices.
- **MODERATE — Inventory Classification:** Current inventories of $90.4M and non-current inventories of $86.3M mean a significant portion of working capital is tied up in stock with slower turnover.
- **MODERATE — NCI/Related Parties:** GMA-related mining contractor costs of $16.8M represent a material related-party transaction that requires ongoing governance scrutiny.

### 11.3 Market and Regulatory Risks
- **MODERATE — Commodity Price Dependency:** Revenue is overwhelmingly gold-price driven. A sustained gold price correction would compress revenue faster than costs.
- **MODERATE — Reclamation and Commitments:** Unrecognized contractual commitments total $26.9M, and reclamation/post-mining guarantees aggregate several million dollars through 2025.
- **LOW — FX Exposure:** Foreign currency net liability declined to $17.0M from $46.8M, but IDR/USD volatility remains.

### 11.4 Valuation Concerns
No market data was provided, preventing any multiple-based assessment. With approximately 24.8 billion shares outstanding, even small price movements would create large market-cap swings, and liquidity may be constrained.

## 14. Investment Thesis

### 14.1 Bull Case
If gold prices remain elevated and ARCI hits its 2026 production target, the operating leverage is substantial. Volume growth combined with sustained pricing could drive full-year revenue and cash generation well above prior-year levels, allowing accelerated deleveraging. Long-dated debt maturities provide runway, and the 42.8% gross margin offers a wide buffer.

### 14.2 Bear Case
A gold price correction would simultaneously crush revenue and erode the 42.8% gross margin if unit costs are sticky. Interest expense of $9.0M per quarter would then dominate cash flows, forcing working capital stress or refinancing. The 1.02x current ratio offers no cushion.

### 14.3 Key Catalysts
- Q2 2026 production volume and realized gold price.
- Drawdown or repayment on the Mandiri treasury line (max $278.6M).
- Resolution of the $17.1M tax claims receivable.
- Any update to 2026 cost guidance or capex budget.
