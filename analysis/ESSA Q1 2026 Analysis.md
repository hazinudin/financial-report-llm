# PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk (ESSA) — Q1 2026
---
## 1. Executive Summary

PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk (ESSA) delivered an exceptionally strong Q1 2026 performance, marked by a **36.8% year-over-year revenue increase to $95.21 million** and a **150.5% surge in net profit to $26.76 million**. The dramatic improvement was driven by a confluence of favorable factors: a 34% YoY increase in ammonia prices to approximately $455/MT, a 16% YoY increase in ammonia production volumes with plant utilization reaching 121%, and the complete elimination of bank debt which slashed finance costs by 66%. The company now operates from a fortress balance sheet position — zero bank debt, a current ratio of 11.46x, and $164.56 million in cash — providing exceptional financial flexibility. However, a scheduled 35–40 day ammonia plant turnaround in May 2026 will create a significant headwind for Q2 results, and the concentration risk in ammonia (which dominates profitability) remains a structural consideration. The stock trades at approximately IDR 880–955 per share, with Indo Premier Sekuritas maintaining a BUY rating and a target price of IDR 1,200, implying meaningful upside from current levels.

**Investment Rating: BUY** — Strong operational momentum, debt-free balance sheet, favorable ammonia pricing environment, and strategic positioning in blue ammonia/SAF provide a compelling risk-reward profile, tempered by near-term turnaround impact and commodity price cyclicality. **Meridian Research positions ESSA as their "champion stock"** — sole listed ammonia producer in Indonesia, de-levered, best-in-class utilization, with enormous tailwind from US-Iran war supply shock.

## 2. Revenue Analysis

### 2.1 Revenue Performance

| Metric | Q1 2026 | Q1 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | $95,213,790 | $69,622,293 | **+36.77%** |

The 36.77% revenue increase is the most significant top-line growth ESSA has delivered in recent quarters and represents a sharp acceleration from the revenue declines experienced through much of FY2025 (where full-year revenue fell 2.12% to ~$295 million).

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price

Based on external disclosures and industry data, the revenue surge was driven by a combination of **both price and volume**:

**Ammonia Segment (Primary Driver):**
- Ammonia selling price increased **34% YoY** to approximately $455/MT in Q1 2026
- See [[Ammonia]] for full market price trends — prices surged an additional **23.3% in April 2026** to $0.37/kg post-Q1
- Ammonia production volume increased **16% YoY** due to resolution of upstream gas supply disruptions that had impacted H1 2025
- Plant utilization rate reached **121%** in Q1 2026 vs. 104% in Q1 2025, reflecting the resolution of gas supply issues from late Q2 2025
- Ammonia shipment volumes also increased, supported by inventory drawdowns from prior periods

**LPG Segment:**
- LPG selling price declined **14% YoY** but recovered **11% QoQ** to approximately $537/MT in Q1 2026
- LPG production was impacted by lower feed gas composition percentages, though this was partially offset by price recovery
- LPG remains a smaller contributor to overall profitability compared to ammonia

**Revenue Quality Assessment:**
- Cash received from customers: $119,311,820 vs. revenue of $95,213,790 — the $24.1 million excess indicates strong cash collection, likely including receivables from Q4 2025
- This cash-to-revenue ratio of 1.25x is excellent and signals robust billing and collection practices
- The increase in trade receivables (from $30.98M to $31.57M) was modest relative to the revenue increase, suggesting healthy receivables management

### 2.3 Revenue Composition Observations

- The ammonia segment is clearly the dominant revenue and profit driver, consistent with ESSA's strategic focus on maximizing ammonia production and pricing
- Related-party receivables ($25.0M) significantly exceed third-party receivables ($6.6M), suggesting substantial intercompany transactions, likely related to PAU (the ammonia subsidiary)
- The revenue concentration in ammonia creates both upside (to rising ammonia prices) and downside risk (to ammonia price declines)

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

- **Near-term headwind:** The scheduled 35–40 day plant turnaround in May 2026 will significantly reduce Q2 2026 ammonia production and sales volumes
- **Medium-term tailwind:** Post-turnaround, management expects approximately 9% production improvement, with target daily production of 2,154 MTPD and total annual production of 723,697 MT for 2026
- **Pricing tailwind:** US-Iran War has created a three-vector supply shock (SoH closure, LNG stranded, plant shutdowns). Ammonia (ME) surged from $480/t to $700/t (+46%) in two months; urea from $490/t to $907/t (+85%). Indo Premier notes a 50% surge in urea and ammonia prices since the conflict escalated. **Meridian Research expects ammonia to catch up to urea pricing** as prolonged LNG outages hit production (urea-ammonia spread at ~$200/t premium expected to mean-revert). India's emergency tender of 2.5MT at $935-960/t (vs. $510/t two months ago) demonstrates desperate demand to secure fertilizer.
- **Rupiah tailwind:** USDIDR hit all-time high near 17,300 (smashing 1998 record of 16,745). ESSA reports in USD but has significant IDR-denominated costs — weak Rupiah is a **net positive** for margins. Meridian Research's average entry on USDIDR is ~16,850 and they continue adding to the position.
- **LPG outlook:** 2026 production target of 62,222 MT with average daily production of 174 MTPD, subject to gas feed composition variability

## 11. Concerns and Risk Factors

### 11.1 Near-Term Risks

1. **Plant Turnaround Impact (HIGH SEVERITY):** The scheduled 35–40 day ammonia plant turnaround in May 2026 will significantly reduce Q2 2026 production and revenue. Based on the 121% utilization rate and ~$95.2M quarterly revenue, the lost production could reduce Q2 revenue by approximately $30–40M. This is the most significant near-term headwind and will create a visible "air pocket" in the quarterly results.

2. **Ammonia Price Cyclicality (HIGH SEVERITY):** Ammonia is a commodity subject to significant price volatility. The current price of ~$455/MT reflects geopolitical premiums that could reverse. See [[Ammonia]] for price history and risk factors. Historical ammonia prices have ranged from $200/MT to $800+/MT, and ESSA's profitability is highly sensitive to ammonia pricing. A $50/MT decline in ammonia price on 723,697 MT of annual production would reduce revenue by approximately $36.2M.

3. **LPG Price Weakness (MODERATE SEVERITY):** LPG prices declined 14% YoY, and while they recovered 11% QoQ, the LPG segment remains under pricing pressure. LPG pricing is linked to CP Aramco benchmarks, which are influenced by global oil and gas dynamics beyond ESSA's control.

4. **Gas Feed Supply Risk (MODERATE SEVERITY):** The H1 2025 gas supply disruptions that reduced production demonstrate ESSA's vulnerability to upstream feedstock availability. Any recurrence of gas supply issues could significantly impact production volumes and revenue.

### 11.2 Structural Risks

5. **NCI Profit Dilution (MODERATE SEVERITY):** The 30.003% NCI in PAU means that approximately 30% of the ammonia profits flow to minority shareholders, not to ESSA shareholders. This is a permanent structural feature that reduces the effective economic interest in ESSA's primary profit driver. NCI receives $0.30 of every $1.00 of PAU profit.

6. **Revenue Concentration in Ammonia (HIGH SEVERITY):** The ammonia segment dominates ESSA's profitability, making the company a single-commodity proxy. Any disruption to ammonia production (turnaround, gas supply, technical issues) or pricing would have an outsized impact on results.

7. **Related-Party Transaction Concentration (MODERATE SEVERITY):** Related-party receivables of $25.0M represent 79.2% of total trade receivables. While this is typical for companies with dominant subsidiaries, it raises governance and transfer pricing concerns that investors should monitor.

8. **Deferred Tax Liability Growth (LOW-MODERATE SEVERITY):** The deferred tax liability increased by $7.43M (18.8%) in one quarter to $46.85M, now representing 64.7% of total liabilities. While this is a non-cash item, it reflects the growing gap between accounting and tax bases, particularly related to PPE revaluation surplus. The reclassification of revaluation surplus to retained earnings at $670,832 per period will gradually reduce this over time.

9. **Low Capex Relative to Depreciation (MODERATE SEVERITY):** Quarterly capex of $1.48M is significantly below quarterly depreciation of approximately $11.4M, implying an under-investment in asset replacement. While the KBR technology plant is relatively modern, sustained under-investment could lead to reliability issues and accelerate the need for major maintenance spending.

10. **Indonesian Rupiah / USD Exchange Rate (TAILWIND, NOT RISK):** ESSA reports in USD but operates in Indonesia with significant IDR-denominated costs (labor, local services, some feedstock). The Rupiah has collapsed to all-time highs near 17,300 USDIDR (smashing the 1998 record of 16,745), driven by quasi-fiscal programs (MBG), foreign SBN outflows (IDR 38.3Tn over past year), and eroding confidence in government policymaking. **For ESSA, this is a significant tailwind** — IDR-denominated costs are cheaper in USD terms while ammonia revenue is USD-linked. Meridian Research is long USDIDR (average entry ~16,850) and expects further depreciation. A strengthening IDR would be the actual risk to margins.

### 11.3 Market and Regulatory Risks

11. **Geopolitical Risk to Ammonia Pricing (MODERATE SEVERITY):** The US-Iran War and Strait of Hormuz closure are the primary drivers of current ammonia prices. **Meridian Research's key risk is "the opening of SoH (real opening)"** — they don't see it reopening anytime soon. If SoH does reopen, ammonia prices could normalize by 30-50% (from $700/t back toward $480/t). However, even partial de-escalation would take at least one month for supply normalization. ESSA benefits from the weak Rupiah (USDIDR at 17,300) which offsets some price risk since costs are IDR-denominated while revenue is USD-linked.

12. **Regulatory Risk (LOW SEVERITY):** Changes in Indonesian energy policy, gas pricing regulations, or environmental requirements could impact ESSA's cost structure or market access. The company notes no significant climate change risks as of March 31, 2026, but this could change with evolving regulations.

13. **Blue Ammonia Execution Risk (LOW-MODERATE SEVERITY):** The blue ammonia initiative is still in the feasibility study stage. Commercialization would require significant capital investment in CCUS technology, regulatory approvals, and offtake agreements. The timeline and economics remain uncertain.

14. **SAF Business Risk (LOW SEVERITY):** ESM's SAF business is in early stages with no current revenue contribution. The SAF market is nascent, and commercialization will require significant investment and execution over multiple years.

### 11.4 Valuation Concerns

15. **Premium Valuation Relative to Commodity Cyclicals (MODERATE SEVERITY):** At a TTM P/E of ~24.45x and P/B of ~2.21x, ESSA trades at a premium to typical commodity chemical companies. While the debt-free balance sheet and growth options justify some premium, the valuation embeds expectations of sustained high ammonia prices and operational performance.

16. **Low Dividend Yield (LOW SEVERITY):** At approximately 1.04–1.12%, the dividend yield is modest and may not attract income-focused investors despite the company's strong cash generation capacity.

17. **Insider Selling (LOW-MODERATE SEVERITY):** Simply Wall St notes "significant insider selling over the past 3 months," which could signal that insiders perceive the stock as fully valued or could reflect personal financial planning needs.

## 14. Investment Thesis

### 14.1 Bull Case

ESSA is a high-quality, debt-free commodity chemical company at a favorable point in the ammonia cycle. The Q1 2026 results demonstrate powerful operating leverage — a 36.77% revenue increase drove 150.51% net profit growth. The company's fortress balance sheet (zero bank debt, $164.6M cash, 11.46x current ratio) provides exceptional financial flexibility and downside protection. **Meridian Research positions ESSA as their "champion stock"** — sole listed ammonia producer in Indonesia, de-levered, best-in-class utilization rates, with enormous macroeconomic tailwinds. The US-Iran War has created a three-vector supply shock (SoH closure blocking 30% of global ammonia/urea trade, 20% of LNG stranded, multiple plant shutdowns globally) driving ammonia prices from $480/t to $700/t (+46%) with further upside expected as ammonia catches up to urea ($907/t). The weak Rupiah (USDIDR at 17,300) provides additional margin tailwind since ESSA reports in USD but has IDR-denominated costs. ESSA's strategic positioning in blue ammonia and SAF provides long-term optionality that is not yet priced into the stock. At a forward P/E of approximately 13x (based on annualized Q1 earnings, adjusted for turnaround), the stock offers attractive value for a company with this balance sheet quality and growth trajectory. Indo Premier's target of IDR 1,200 implies approximately 36% upside from current levels. **Meridian's summary: "Long: USD, ESSA. Short/Avoid: JCI."**

### 14.2 Bear Case

ESSA is fundamentally a commodity cyclical whose profitability is heavily dependent on ammonia prices.
