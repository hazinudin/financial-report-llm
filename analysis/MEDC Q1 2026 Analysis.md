## Cost Concerns

### Rising Cost Pressures

Despite the profit surge, cost inflation remains a concern:

1. **Crude Oil Purchases**: +122.5% ($46M → $103M)
   - Likely reflects higher trading volumes or price increases
   - Needs monitoring for sustainability

2. **Production & Lifting Costs**: +31.5% ($100M → $132M)
   - Outpacing revenue growth
   - FIBI/Corridor integration costs may be a factor

3. **DD&A**: +14.8% ($149M → $171M)
   - Higher asset base from FIBI acquisition
   - Expected to remain elevated

Despite higher absolute gross profit, the margin compression indicates cost pressures are real. The net profit improvement came primarily from **operating leverage and associates' recovery**, not operational efficiency gains.
