# PT Central Omega Resources Tbk (DKFT) — Q1 2026
---

## 1. Executive Summary
PT Central Omega Resources Tbk (DKFT) delivered a robust Q1 2026 performance characterized by significant margin expansion and aggressive deleveraging. Revenue grew by 20.2%, reaching IDR 506.01 trillion, while net profit surged by 74.1% to IDR 237.73 trillion. This growth was driven primarily by the Nickel segment, which remains the company's core engine, benefiting from a substantial reduction in COGS as a percentage of revenue.

The balance sheet has undergone a structural shift toward a net-cash position. The company successfully repaid IDR 348.69 billion in loans during the quarter, including the full repayment of the Bank China Construction Bank Indonesia Tbk facility on February 5, 2026. Consequently, the net debt to equity ratio has swung from a positive 11.84% at prior year-end to a negative 54.79%, indicating a massive liquidity cushion.

Valuation remains anchored by high profitability ratios, with an annualized ROE of 70.6% and an ROA of 30.8%. While market data for P/E and P/B is not provided in the current stub, the fundamental trajectory is strongly bullish, supported by a cash conversion ratio of 1.39x and a healthy current ratio of 3.14x.

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price
Revenue growth was driven by the Nickel segment, which generated IDR 494.78 trillion in external sales. Production volumes across key blocks remain substantial:
- **IMN Lambolo Block:** 299,767 MT
- **BKA Konawe Blok 1:** 138,553 MT
- **MPR Morowali Blok 2:** 116,056 MT

The increase in revenue relative to the prior period suggests a favorable price environment or increased throughput, as the company maintained a strong external sales focus in the Nickel segment.

### 2.3 Revenue Composition Observations
The revenue stream is highly concentrated among a few key customers:
- **PT Sino Indo Nickel:** IDR 277.48 trillion (55.0% of sales)
- **PT Megah Surya Pertiwi:** IDR 165.06 trillion (33.0% of sales)
- **PT Vantama Arja Sejahtera:** IDR 110.65 trillion (26.0% of sales)
- **PT Five Star General Resources:** IDR 52.23 trillion (10.0% of sales)

*Note: The percentage totals exceed 100%, indicating overlapping reporting or multi-product procurement by these entities.*

The business is almost entirely dependent on Nickel, with the Limestone segment contributing only IDR 11.23 trillion in external sales and operating at a net loss of IDR 1.22 trillion.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook
Sustainability is underpinned by significant residual reserves:
- **MPR Morowali Blok 1:** 20.40 million MT
- **IMN Morowali Blok 3:** 24.39 million MT
- **IMN Lambolo Block:** 21.39 million MT
- **BKA Konawe Blok 3:** 8.88 million MT

The massive reserve base provides a long-term runway. However, the high customer concentration (Sino Indo Nickel and Megah Surya Pertiwi) represents a critical dependency. Any shift in procurement strategy by these two players would materially impact the top line.

## 14. Investment Thesis

### 14.1 Bull Case
Nickel prices remain stable or rise, and the company continues to convert high margins into cash. The transition to a net-cash company removes insolvency risk and allows for increased dividends or strategic acquisitions in the smelting space.

### 14.2 Bear Case
A major customer (e.g., PT Sino Indo Nickel) defaults or reduces volumes, leading to a collapse in revenue and a spike in bad debt (already evidenced by the IDR 61.98B impairment allowance).

### 14.3 Key Catalysts
- Further expansion of CORII smelting capacity.
- Diversification of the customer base to reduce concentration.
- Announcement of a special dividend given the net cash position.

### 14.4 Recommendation
**BUY (Risk: Moderate)**
The company is a cash cow with an exceptional ROE and a now-negligible debt burden. The risk of customer concentration is the only significant deterrent.
- **Target Price Range:** Based on BVPS of IDR 266.62 and 70% ROE, a premium to book is justified.
- **Review Trigger:** Any drop in the collections ratio below 50% or a change in the status of the top two customers.
