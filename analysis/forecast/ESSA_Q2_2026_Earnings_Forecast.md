# PT ESSA Industries Indonesia Tbk (ESSA) — Q2 2026 Earnings Forecast

## Executive Summary

ESSA enters Q2 2026 facing a **dual-headed earnings challenge**: a scheduled 35–40 day ammonia plant turnaround in May that removes roughly 40% of quarterly production capacity, paired with a sharp June reversal in ammonia prices following the US-Iran MOU and Strait of Hormuz reopening. Despite these headwinds, the quarter benefits from the **April–May price peak** (the highest ammonia prices since the war began on February 28) and a **weak Rupiah tailwind** (USDIDR at all-time highs near 17,300) that continues to boost USD-denominated margins.

**Forecast Summary (Base Case):**

| Metric | Q1 2026 (Actual) | Q2 2026 (Base Case) | QoQ Change | Q2 2025 (Est.) | YoY Change |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|------------|----------------|------------|
| **Revenue** | $95.21M | **~$82M** | -13.9% | ~$63M | +30% |
| **Gross Profit** | $43.64M | **~$36M** | -17.5% | ~$21M | +71% |
| **Gross Margin** | 45.82% | **~44%** | -180 bps | ~33% | +1,100 bps |
| **Operating Profit** | $33.95M | **~$26M** | -23.4% | ~$12M | +117% |
| **Net Profit** | $26.76M | **~$21M** | -21.5% | ~$8M | +163% |
| **Net Margin** | 28.10% | **~25.5%** | -260 bps | ~13% | +1,250 bps |
| **EPS (owners)** | $1.089 | **~$0.76** | -30.2% | ~$0.33 | +130% |

**Investment Rating: HOLD (near-term) / BUY (post-Q2 dip)** — The Q2 earnings dip is well-telegraphed and fully expected by the market. The critical question is not the Q2 print itself but whether the **post-turnaround production recovery (+9%)** and **elevated contract pricing** can offset the declining ammonia spot price trend in H2 2026. The base case forecast of ~$21M net profit (down 22% QoQ but up 163% YoY) represents a healthy earnings floor given the operational headwinds.

**Assumptions:**

- Ammonia realized price: ~$560/MT (contract pricing captures most of the April–May peak; June decline has limited impact on Q2 average due to contract lag)
- Ammonia sales volume: ~150,000 MT (production ~130,000 MT + ~20,000 MT inventory drawdown)
- LPG revenue: ~$10.5M (slight price improvement)
- Turnaround costs: ~$5M expensed (efficient execution)
- Post-turnaround production improvement begins late June

**Profitability:**

- EPS (owners): ~$1.02

**Rationale:** In this scenario, ESSA's contract pricing formula (referencing Middle East FOB or a regional index with a lag) captures the bulk of the April–May ammonia price surge (ME FOB peaked at ~$700/MT). The gross margin actually *expands* slightly because the incremental revenue from higher prices flows at near-100% margin given the fixed cost structure. This scenario is plausible if Genesis Corporation's offtake pricing formula has a short (1-month) lag and references ME FOB benchmarks.

**Probability assessment (20%):** Requires three favorable outcomes simultaneously: (1) contract captures most of the spot surge, (2) turnaround completes on the short end of guidance, and (3) significant inventory drawdown cushions volume. Each individually is plausible, but the conjunction is less likely.

**Rationale:** In this scenario, the contract pricing formula lags significantly, and the June price collapse (NE Asia fell from $0.38/kg to $0.33/kg, a -13.2% MoM decline) pulls the Q2 average down. However, even in this bear case, Q2 2026 net profit (~$10M) exceeds Q2 2025 (~$8M) due to the absence of gas supply issues and zero debt.

**Probability assessment (30%):** Higher than the bull case because the June price reversal is a confirmed event (not a risk — it has already happened), and turnarounds of 5-year-old plants (last turnaround was 2021) typically uncover additional maintenance items. The contract pricing lag is the key swing factor.

## 4. Ammonia Price Analysis

### Realized Price Estimation

ESSA's ammonia is sold under a long-term offtake agreement with **Genesis Corporation** (Japan), a related party under common control (Laroya family). The pricing formula was amended in January 2023 to be "**aligned to regional price index**" (per Q4 2025 Analysis). The exact index and lag period are not publicly disclosed, creating the **largest single uncertainty** in this forecast.

**Q1 2026 price benchmarking:**

| Reference | Q1 2026 Average | ESSA Realized | Differential |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------|-------------|
| NE Asia spot (IMARC) | ~$305/MT ($0.30/kg) | $455/MT | +$150/MT premium |
| Middle East FOB | ~$510/MT (est.) | $455/MT | -$55/MT discount |

The data suggests ESSA's contract references a **Middle East or blended regional benchmark** rather than NE Asia spot, with a modest FOB Indonesia discount. The $455/MT realized in Q1 (when the war only affected March) is consistent with a pre-war ME FOB of ~$480/MT less a ~$25–55/MT logistics/quality differential.

**Q2 2026 market prices:**

| Period | NE Asia (USD/kg) | NE Asia ($/MT) | Middle East FOB ($/MT) |
|-------|------------------|----------------|----------------------|
| April (peak) | 0.37 | $370 | ~$700 |
| May (lagged peak) | ~0.38 | ~$380 | ~$700 |
| June (post-MOU) | 0.33 | $330 | ~$570 |
| **Q2 average** | **~0.36** | **~$360** | **~$657** |

**Q2 2026 realized price scenarios:**

| Scenario | Contract Assumption | Q2 Realized | vs. Q1 ($455) |
|----------|---------------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Bull** | ME FOB - $50 discount, 1-mo lag | ~$560/MT | +23% |
| **Base** | Blended index, 1–2 mo lag | ~$510/MT | +12% |
| **Bear** | NE Asia + premium, 2-mo lag | ~$470/MT | +3% |

**Key insight:** Even in the bear case, Q2 realized price exceeds Q1 because the war-driven price surge began affecting benchmarks in March (captured in Q2 with a lag). The June spot decline does not fully flow through to Q2 realized pricing due to contract lag effects. However, the **June decline is a leading indicator of Q3 2026 pricing pressure** — if the SoH reopening continues and prices fall further, Q3 realized prices could drop below Q1 levels.

### Price Sensitivity

A $10/MT change in realized ammonia price on ~140,000 MT of Q2 sales volume impacts revenue by ~$1.4M and net profit by ~$1.1M (after tax and NCI). The $90/MT spread between bear ($470) and bull ($560) scenarios translates to a ~$12.6M revenue swing and ~$9.5M net profit swing — underscoring why **contract pricing is the dominant variable** in this forecast.
