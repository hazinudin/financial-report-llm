# PT Bekasi Fajar Industrial Estate Tbk (BEST) — Q1 2026
---

*All figures in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) full. The financial statements are unaudited interim consolidated statements for the three months ended 31 March 2026. Income statement and cash flow comparatives refer to the three months ended 31 March 2025; balance sheet comparatives refer to 31 December 2025.*

## 1. Executive Summary

BEST swung from a deep year-ago loss to profitability in Q1 2026, reporting revenue of IDR 89,516,140,596 (+96.9% YoY) and net profit of IDR 9,263,991,463 versus a net loss of IDR 33,166,275,694 in Q1 2025. Gross margin expanded to 65.3% from an implied 55.7% in the prior-year quarter, driven by operating leverage on industrial estate land sales where incremental revenue carries low marginal cost. Operating profit is not separately disclosed in the interim filing, but the reported pre-tax margin of 12.4% and net margin of 10.3% confirm the structural turnaround, even as a negative effective tax rate (-16.7%) and a 62% year-on-year collapse in selling expense raise unresolved questions about the quality and sustainability of the Q1 earnings mix.

The balance sheet remains fortress-like. Total assets were effectively flat at IDR 5.81 trillion, equity rose 0.2% quarter-on-quarter to IDR 4.47 trillion, and the current ratio stands at 5.6x with cash of IDR 371 billion covering 93% of current liabilities. Debt/equity is 0.30x and the group remained in full compliance with Bank Mandiri loan covenants as of 31 March 2026. The group repaid IDR 45.95 billion of principal during the quarter and recorded interest expense down 30% year-on-year, confirming mechanical deleveraging.

Operating cash flow was the standout metric at IDR 71.8 billion, reversing a negative IDR 29.0 billion outflow in Q1 2025, with collections from customers reaching IDR 125.7 billion—a 1.40x collection ratio that suggests strong cash conversion or possible advance receipts. No market data is provided, rendering multiple-based valuation impossible. We assign a **HOLD (Qualified)** rating: the operational inflection is genuine, but the quarter is distorted by tax anomalies, customer concentration, and unaudited interim status. Target price range: **not disclosed** owing to absent market data. Key catalysts are the Q2 2026 earnings release, resolution of the 2017 corporate income tax dispute, and execution of the IDR 600 billion marketing sales target.

## 2. Revenue Analysis

### 2.1 Revenue Performance

| Period | Revenue (IDR) | Prior Period (IDR) | Change |
|--------|---------------|--------------------|--------|
| Q1 2026 | 89,516,140,596 | 45,460,225,118 | +96.91% |

The 96.9% top-line growth is mechanically amplified by the depressed Q1 2025 base, which was marred by weak land sales and high fixed-cost absorption. As noted in the prior [[BEST Q2 2026 Analysis]], first-half 2026 revenue reached IDR 380.4 billion, implying that the vast majority of H1 sales were recognized in Q2 rather than Q1. Consequently, Q1 2026 should be viewed as a lagging, lower-volume quarter within a half-year surge.

### 2.2 Revenue Drivers — Volume vs. Price

The company does not disclose plot-level volume or average selling price in the interim statements. Revenue is derived from two identifiable sources: (i) industrial estate land sales and (ii) recurring income (maintenance fees, service charges, water, and lease). The industrial estate segment contributed IDR 79.6 billion (88.9% of total), while the “Others” segment—hotel and facilities development—contributed IDR 9.9 billion (11.1%). External research indicates that management is targeting IDR 600 billion of marketing sales for full-year 2026, anchored by data center, logistics, and F&B investors ([https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260717/192/1988884/bekasi-fajar-best-bidik-marketing-sales-rp600-miliar-dari-investor-jepang-dan-china](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260717/192/1988884/bekasi-fajar-best-bidik-marketing-sales-rp600-miliar-dari-investor-jepang-dan-china); [https://emitennews.com/news/kinerja-kuartal-i-solid-best-optimistis-laba-tumbuh-dua-digit](https://emitennews.com/news/kinerja-kuartal-i-solid-best-optimistis-laba-tumbuh-dua-digit)). Foreign exchange risk is limited because the group converted all loans to Rupiah in 2023 ([https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/bekasi-fajar-best-mitigasi-dampak-suku-bunga-tinggi-dan-pelemahan-rupiah](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/bekasi-fajar-best-mitigasi-dampak-suku-bunga-tinggi-dan-pelemahan-rupiah)); a 5% Rupiah move impacts PBT by only IDR 1.85 billion per the sensitivity disclosure.

### 2.3 Revenue Composition Observations

Customer concentration is acute. PT Stars Global Resources Indonesia accounted for IDR 35,536,800,000 in Q1 2026, equivalent to roughly 40% of quarterly revenue. No other customer exceeded the 10% disclosure threshold. All operations are domestic, concentrated in Bekasi, West Java; no geographic segments are reported. The segment composition is heavily skewed toward industrial estate land sales, with the hotel/facilities segment contributing just over one-tenth of the top line.

### 2.4 Revenue Sustainability and Forward Outlook

Management’s forward pipeline appears robust. As of April 2026, BEST reportedly held demand for approximately 70 hectares dominated by data center and logistics sectors ([https://konstruksimedia.com/bekasi-fajar-best-kantongi-permintaan-70-hektare-sektor-data-center-dan-logistik-dominasi/](https://konstruksimedia.com/bekasi-fajar-best-kantongi-permintaan-70-hektare-sektor-data-center-dan-logistik-dominasi/); [https://www.beritajejakfakta.id/bekasi-fajar-permintaan-lahan-industri](https://www.beritajejakfakta.id/bekasi-fajar-permintaan-lahan-industri)). The BEFA Industrial Hub (Standard Factory Building) in MM2100 was launched in September 2025 to capture warehouse and logistics demand ([https://mm2100industrialtown.com/pt-bekasi-fajar-industrial-estate-launches-befa-industrial-hub/](https://mm2100industrialtown.com/pt-bekasi-fajar-industrial-estate-launches-befa-industrial-hub/)). However, Q1 2026 revenue of IDR 89.5 billion represents only 14.9% of the IDR 600 billion annual target, implying heavy back-loading and high reliance on a small number of large transactions. Watch items include the timing of the PT Handal Gemilang Sentosa land sale escrow (committed 16 December 2025) and whether Q1 revenue included completion of prior-period pre-sales rather than new marketing activity.

The three subsidiaries collectively hold IDR 2.94 trillion of assets, roughly 51% of the consolidated total. PT Bekasi Matra Industrial Estate is the dominant platform.

### 7.2 The Core Operating Business

The industrial estate segment is the crown jewel. In Q1 2026 it generated external sales of IDR 79.6 billion (88.9% of group revenue), gross profit of IDR 54.8 billion (68.9% segment gross margin), and net profit of IDR 7.9 billion (85.8% of group net profit). Segment assets of IDR 5.62 trillion represent 96.8% of the consolidated balance sheet. No reserve or license disclosures are provided in the interim filing, but the land bank is physically concentrated in Bekasi and largely pledged to Bank Mandiri.

### 7.3 Strategic Question Marks

Three items cloud the asset picture. First, the subsidiary BMIE approved the disposal of fiber-optic equipment to related party PT Kawasan Lintas Biru Digital (carrying value IDR 10.37 billion as of 31 March 2026), but the transaction had not closed by the reporting date. Second, the hotel management agreement between BSN and PT Hospitality & Quality Management was mutually terminated on 30 June 2025, raising questions about the “Others” segment’s revenue stability. Third, the BMIE-KLBD telecom agreement was amended on 25 August 2025 to expire on 30 June 2026, creating near-term renewal risk for ancillary recurring income.

### 7.4 Growth Options

The BEFA Industrial Hub (Standard Factory Building) launched in September 2025 targets logistics and warehouse tenants, diversifying beyond pure land sales. Management’s disclosed pipeline of ~70 hectares for data center, logistics, and F&B investors provides a visible runway. Macro research projects data center and EV demand as new drivers for Indonesian industrial estates in 2026 ([https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/data-center-dan-ev-diproyeksi-jadi-motor-baru-permintaan-kawasan-industri-pada-2026](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/data-center-dan-ev-diproyeksi-jadi-motor-baru-permintaan-kawasan-industri-pada-2026)). These are medium-confidence external tailwinds, not in-period revenue drivers.

### 7.5 Intercompany and Related-Party Dynamics

Related-party transactions with PT Megalopolis Manunggal Industrial Development (same key management) are material: trade receivables IDR 7.23 billion, trade payables IDR 19.88 billion, and cost of revenues IDR 9.01 billion (29.0% of consolidated COGS). Intercompany eliminations net cleanly to the consolidated totals, but the payables concentration raises governance and pricing-risk questions that are not resolved in the interim notes.
