# PT Kalbe Farma Tbk (KLBF) — Q1 2026
---

## 1. Executive Summary

PT Kalbe Farma Tbk (KLBF) reported unaudited consolidated interim results for the three months ended 31 March 2026. The reporting unit is the consolidated entity comprising the parent and 50+ subsidiaries engaged in pharmaceuticals, consumer health, nutritionals, distribution, and digital/healthcare services. Top-line momentum held: revenue reached IDR 9,678,361 million, up 10.13% from IDR 8,788,190 million in Q1 2025 (revenue_growth ratio 0.101292). However, profitability deteriorated — operating profit fell 5.41% to IDR 1,351,876 million (operating_profit_growth -0.054145) and net profit attributable to owners slipped 4.44% to IDR 1,028,927 million, with EPS contracting 3.52% to IDR 22.74 (eps_growth -0.035214). The decoupling of revenue growth from earnings growth is the central tension of the quarter.

Balance-sheet expansion was modest: total assets rose 4.19% to IDR 31,985,237 million (total_assets_growth 0.041887) versus the 31 December 2025 restated position, funded entirely by retained earnings and minority additions rather than debt. The company remains in a pronounced net-cash position. Net debt is negative IDR 4,545,475 million (net_debt_to_equity -0.176628), loans-to-equity is a mere 0.009977, and current ratio stands at 3.556 (current_ratio). Liquidity is therefore not a constraint; the constraint is operating leverage.

Valuation cannot be finalized: the market-data stub supplied no price, market capitalization, or shares-outstanding market figure beyond the computed 45,247.449 million shares implied by EPS. Book value per share is IDR 536.99 (book_value_per_share). With no P/E, P/B, or EV/EBITDA computable (pe_annualized, pb, ev, ev_ebitda all null), we anchor on fundamentals only.

We assign a HOLD rating with moderate risk. The quarter demonstrates durable demand but reveals cost inflation in selling expenses (IDR 1,895,755 million vs IDR 1,757,839 million prior) and inventory build that outpaces sales, pressuring free cash flow conversion (cash_conversion 0.250995). The investment case hinges on whether margin stabilization or further license-driven opex materializes in H2 2026.
