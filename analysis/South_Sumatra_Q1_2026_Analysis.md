# South Sumatra Palm Oil Plantations: Q1 2025 vs Q1 2026 Analysis

## Summary

PTPS (PT Pulau Subur) saw a **-49.6% drop in TBS/FFB production** in Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025 (6,026 → 3,040 tonnes). This prompted an investigation of whether other plantation companies with South Sumatra exposure experienced similar declines, potentially indicating a regional weather/climate factor.

## Companies Without Production Volume Disclosure

GZCO, PNGO, SIMP, and STAA do not disclose TBS/FFB harvest tonnage in quarterly financial statements, making it impossible to attribute revenue changes to production vs. price.

---

## Verdict

| Factor | Evidence |
|--------|----------|
| **Regional production decline** | ✅ Confirmed for PTPS (-50%), ANDI (-30%), SGRO (-13%) — the 3 companies that disclose volume data |
| **CPO price decline** | ✅ Significant factor for ANDI (-56% price drop); PTPS appears volume-driven only |
| **Diversification as protection** | ✅ LSIP, SIMP, STAA showed no revenue decline due to operations outside South Sumatra |
| **Weather as root cause** | ⚠️ Plausible but not proven — consistent pattern across companies, but commodity price effects and company-specific factors also present |

The data is **consistent with** a regional production disruption in South Sumatra during Q1 2026, but the financial reports alone cannot definitively isolate weather as the sole cause without corroborating meteorological data or management commentary on operational conditions.
