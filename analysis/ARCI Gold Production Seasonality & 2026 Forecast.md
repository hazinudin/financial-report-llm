## 1. Executive Summary

ARCI's gold production in 2025 exhibited a **clear seasonal pattern** rather than a flat trajectory. Q1 is consistently the weakest quarter due to Indonesia's rainy season, while Q2–Q3 represent peak production during the dry months. Q1 2026 actual production of **29 koz** (vs 21 koz in Q1 2025) signals a ~38% improvement on the prior-year base, pointing to expanded capacity or operational improvements.

**Management has set an official FY 2026 production target of minimum +15%** (~140 koz), per CEO Rudy Suhendra's public presentation on 7 May 2026 (Kontan, "[[Harga Emas Melonjak, ARCI Bidik Produksi Naik 15% pada 2026]]"). This is notably **below** the seasonal-ratio-derived forecast of 155–165 koz (27–35% growth), suggesting management is guiding conservatively or expects the seasonal uplift to moderate. The company's gold price reference is **US$4,700–5,000/oz**. Two major growth projects — a plant capacity expansion from 4M to 6M tons/year (construction starts H1-2026) and a new underground mine at Pit Marawuwung — underpin production growth beyond 2028.

## 3. Seasonality Analysis

### 3.1 Pattern Confirmation

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Lowest quarter | Q1 (21 koz) |
| Peak quarter | Q3 (37 koz) |
| Q1 vs Peak delta | **-43%** |
| Q1 vs Annual Avg (30.5 koz) | **-31%** |
| Coefficient of Variation | ~24% |

The production pattern is **not flat** — there is meaningful quarterly fluctuation driven by seasonal factors:

1. **Q1 Weakness (Jan–Mar):** Indonesia's peak rainy season in North Sulawesi reduces mining efficiency — pit flooding, haul road degradation, and restricted blasting operations. Q1 2025 production of 21 koz is the annual trough.

2. **Q2 Recovery (Apr–Jun):** Transition to drier conditions enables ramp-up. Q2 production of 32 koz represents a 52% increase over Q1.

3. **Q3 Peak (Jul–Sep):** Dry season maximizes mining throughput. Q3 at 37 koz is the annual peak, ~76% above Q1.

4. **Q4 Moderation (Oct–Dec):** Early rains return, production moderates to 32 koz. Year-end maintenance and inventory management may also contribute.

## 4. Q1 2026 Actual — The Inflection Point

From [[ARCI Q1 2026 report]] (`results/ARCI_Q1_2026.md`):

> *"MSM and TTN's total gold production for the current period was approximately **29 kilo ounces** (unaudited). The accumulated total gold production since the start of production activities up to the date of the statement of financial position was approximately 2,546 kilo ounces."*

### 4.1 Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025 Comparison

| Metric | Q1 2025 | Q1 2026 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| Production (koz) | 21 | 29 | **+38%** |
| Revenue (US$'000) | 90,777 | 136,908 | +50.8% |
| Gross Profit (US$'000) | 25,937 | 58,627 | +126.0% |
| Gross Margin | 28.6% | 42.8% | +14.2pp |
| Net Profit (US$'000) | 10,483 | 30,189 | +188.0% |

The 38% production increase in Q1 2026 is a significant improvement. The even larger revenue (+50.8%) and gross profit (+126%) gains reflect both higher volumes and higher realized gold prices.

### 4.2 What Q1 2026 Tells Us

The stronger Q1 baseline is now confirmed by management (per Kontan, May 2026):
- **Capacity expansion confirmed** — Plant expansion from 4M to 6M tons/year underway; underground mining at Kopra running smoothly, Pit Marawuwung starting in 2026
- **Operational improvements** — Better wet-season mining practices, drainage infrastructure
- **Higher-grade ore** from underground mining operations
- **Accumulated production** now at 2,546 koz (vs 2,415 koz at end-Q1 2025), adding 131 koz in 12 months
- **Gold sales volume** of 22 koz in Q1 2026 (+11% YoY), suggesting some inventory build at higher prices

## 5. FY 2026 Production Forecast

### 5.1 Methodology

Two approaches are applied:

**Approach A — Seasonal Ratios from Q1 2026 Base:**
Using Q1 2026 actual (29 koz) and applying 2025 seasonal ratios (Q2/Q1 = 1.52x, Q3/Q1 = 1.76x, Q4/Q1 = 1.52x):

| Quarter | Forecast (koz) | Basis |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Q1 2026 | 29 | Actual |
| Q2 2026 | 44 | 29 × 1.52 |
| Q3 2026 | 51 | 29 × 1.76 |
| Q4 2026 | 44 | 29 × 1.52 |
| **FY 2026** | **168** | |

**Approach B — Conservative YoY Growth:**
Applying the Q1 growth rate of 38% to each 2025 quarter, then moderating for seasonality normalization:

| Quarter | 2025 Actual | +38% Growth | Moderated Forecast (koz) |
|---------|------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Q1 | 21 | 29 | 29 (actual) |
| Q2 | 32 | 44 | 42 |
| Q3 | 37 | 51 | 47 |
| Q4 | 32 | 44 | 42 |
| **FY 2026** | **122** | **168** | **160** |

**Approach C — Management Official Guidance:**
Per CEO Rudy Suhendra's public presentation (7 May 2026, Kontan), ARCI targets **minimum 15% production growth** for FY 2026. This implies:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| FY 2025 Base | 122 koz |
| +15% Target | **~140 koz** |
| Implied Quarterly Avg | ~35 koz |

> *Note: The "minimum 15%" language suggests management sees 140 koz as a floor rather than a ceiling. The Q1 2026 actual of 29 koz (which is already +36% YoY vs Q1 2025) is consistent with a trajectory that could exceed the +15% target.*

### 5.2 Forecast Range

| Scenario | FY 2026 Forecast (koz) | YoY Growth | Source |
|----------|----------------------|------------|--------|
| Management Floor | 140 | +15% | Official guidance |
| Conservative | 150 | +23% | Below seasonal ratios |
| Base Case | 160 | +31% | Seasonal ratio + moderation |
| Optimistic | 168 | +38% | Full seasonal extrapolation |

**Revised Base Case: ~150–160 koz for FY 2026** (bracketed between management guidance of 140 koz and seasonal extrapolation of 168 koz). Management's +15% target likely embeds conservatism; the actual H1-2026 run rate will be the key differentiator.

### 5.3 Revenue Implication (Revised July 2026)

Management's previous reference price of US$4,700–5,000/oz (May 2026) has been invalidated by the July 2026 correction. Per the [[Gold Market Research Briefing]], spot gold is now ~$4,176/oz. The ~$500/oz correction from May levels erases ~$80M in expected annual revenue. Revised scenarios:

**At revised base case (US$4,200/oz — current H2 assumption):**

| Metric | FY 2025 Actual | FY 2026 Forecast | Change |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|--------|
| Production (koz) | 122 | 150–160 | +23–31% |
| Avg Gold Price (US$/oz) | ~3,200 | ~4,200 | +31% |
| Est. Revenue (US$M) | 496 | ~630–672 | +27–35% |

**At bear case (US$3,900/oz — if Fed hikes materialize):**

| Metric | FY 2025 Actual | FY 2026 Forecast | Change |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|--------|
| Production (koz) | 122 | 150–160 | +23–31% |
| Avg Gold Price (US$/oz) | ~3,200 | ~3,900 | +22% |
| Est. Revenue (US$M) | 496 | ~585–624 | +18–26% |

Revenue growth is still driven by a **dual tailwind** of volume (+23–31%) and price (+22–31%), though the price contribution has narrowed significantly from the May outlook. The downside risk is margin compression: at $4,200/oz, ARCI remains in the top royalty bracket (16%), but the lower realized price compresses unit margins versus the May scenario.

## 6. Key Assumptions & Risks

### 6.1 Assumptions
- Seasonal pattern remains consistent with 2025
- No major operational disruptions (weather, equipment, labor)
- Gold price remains in the **US$3,900–4,500/oz range** (revised July 2026: spot ~$4,176; previous management reference of $4,700+ invalidated by Fed hawkish pivot)
- No regulatory changes affecting production quotas
- Plant capacity expansion (4M → 6M tons/yr) construction begins on schedule in H1-2026

### 6.2 Upside Risks
- Gold prices exceeding US$5,000/oz could incentivize maximum throughput and justify accelerated capex
- Successful exploration results adding higher-grade ore to the mine plan (ARCI is actively expanding geophysics studies)
- Further operational efficiency improvements reducing wet-season impact
- **Plant capacity expansion (4M → 6M tons/year)** — if completed ahead of schedule, could add upside to 2026 output, though primary benefit is post-2028
- **New underground mine at Pit Marawuwung** — following successful Kopra underground mine launch, adds incremental high-grade ore access

### 6.3 Downside Risks
- **Extreme weather events** — La Niña patterns could extend the rainy season, depressing Q1/Q4 output
- **Regulatory changes & Margin Compression** — Indonesia's progressive royalty regime (7–16%) hits the top bracket at prices above ~$4,000/oz. With gold currently ~$4,176, ARCI remains in the top bracket, but the lower realized price compresses unit margins significantly versus the May 2026 scenario.
- **Mine reserve constraints** — Latest published reserves are from 2020 (3,884 koz); depletion risk at 160 koz/yr implies a ~24-year reserve life, but new reserve estimates may differ
- **Equipment/maintenance issues** — Higher throughput increases wear on processing infrastructure
- **Execution risk on expansion projects** — Plant upgrade and underground mining represent significant capex and operational complexity
- **Cost inflation** — Mirae Asset Sekuritas noted that operational efficiency and cost control must be maintained during production ramp-up

The FY 2025 production of 122 koz was only ~31% above FY 2024's 93 koz, yet revenue surged 72.5% due to gold price appreciation. ## 9. Conclusion

ARCI's gold production follows a **clear seasonal pattern** with Q1 as the trough (~0.69x average) and Q3 as the peak (~1.21x average). The pattern is driven by Indonesia's monsoon cycle affecting open-pit mining operations in North Sulawesi.

The Q1 2026 actual of 29 koz (+36% YoY) is the strongest Q1 on record and signals a structural step-up in production capacity. **Management has officially guided to minimum +15% growth (~140 koz), while seasonal extrapolation suggests 155–168 koz is achievable. The revised base case is 150–160 koz.**

Two major catalysts underpin long-term growth: the plant capacity expansion from 4M to 6M tons/year (construction starting H1-2026) and new underground mining at Pit Marawuwung. However, the July 2026 gold price correction (to ~$4,176/oz) has reduced the revenue outlook. At the revised base case price of ~$4,200/oz, **revenue is now projected at ~US$630–670M in FY 2026** (vs. previous ~$700-750M estimates), still representing a 27–35% increase over FY 2025's US$496M, driven primarily by volume growth.
